# Alte Post and the Bernstein house in Steglitz

## Alte Post, Bergstraße 1

The former Postamt Steglitz I occupies Bergstraße 1 and Heesestraße 13–14. Wilhelm Walter designed the building for the postal administration in 1907–1909. Notenkind lists Bergstraße 1 as its current address. Its mapped point sits within the complex near the courtyard. The model depicts the exterior and places no speculative school sign on the historic portal. [Berlin heritage record 09065480](https://denkmaldatenbank.berlin.de/daobj.php?obj_dok_nr=09065480) · [Notenkind directions](https://www.notenkind.de/anfahrt.html) · [Mapped music school](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/5393857519)

The red masonry, stone base, pointed portals, tracery and the green copper bell and lantern follow the photographed building. The roof form uses the measured turret plan and maximum height. Details of the copper profile, window spacing and ornament are visual estimates. ## F. W. Bernstein, Südendstraße 2

The Berlin heritage register identifies the 1899–1900 residential building at Südendstraße 2. Bernstein describes moving into Südendstraße in 1985, and the plaque at number 2 names him and confirms the address. [Berlin heritage record 09065660](https://denkmaldatenbank.berlin.de/daobj.php?obj_dok_nr=09065660) · [Bernstein’s essay](https://jungle.world/artikel/1997/48/wo-ich-wohne) · [Mapped footprint](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/113993817)

The model follows the rose plaster, rusticated base, pair of projecting oriels, stone balusters, dark portal with a fanlight and central gable decoration. The factory uses the actual north-facing street wall in each mode and retains the existing body and roof. The plaque photo establishes a silver plate on a low moulded band. Its position along that band is estimated because the photographs show no wider location on the frontage. The model renders the name without the literary quotation.
