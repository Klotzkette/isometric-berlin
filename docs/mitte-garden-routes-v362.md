## Road markings opposite the Dom

Two mapped hatched areas mark the median of the Schloßplatz carriageway, with an open crossing between them. The represented part is the eastern, straight section of the long marking and the complete smaller marking on the other side of the crossing. Their mapped outlines and the orthophoto agree. The display covers 212.49 m² of asphalt. All 35 stripe centres were identified from brightness peaks in the corresponding rectified image, with the fitted slope recorded separately per marking. The 12 cm border and 24 cm stripe widths are display estimates. The actual western cut of the work area remains open. Three previous inferred median curb lines yielded just their conflicting segments, with the original coordinates of every affected line retained. Twenty-five nearby native curb cells were lowered to road height within the same verified asphalt. The crossing and travel lanes stay open. ## Biergarten and park approaches

Two compacted paths run through the landscaped area at the Café am Neuen See in the Großer Tiergarten. Their mapped centre lines run for 49.7 m and 108.5 m within the garden. A third path alongside the garden contributes 1.6 m² at its edge. All three match the complete older reserved approach courses and their current OSM surface tags. The displayed path coverage is 347.18 m² in Drawn and 313.75 m² in the block style. Widths retain the earlier 2.2 m estimate. The compilation cuts out buildings, water, timber decking and previous garden floors. The new surface meets the original furniture setting and reuses the original café mesh batches. The current view of James June beside the Computerspielemuseum was also checked against the operator’s description of the reopened summer garden and its winter closure. ## Reproduction

Run `python scripts/build_mitte_garden_routes_v362.py` with the project environment. Exact map requests, hash digests, clipped working boundaries, source versions and observed stripe endpoints are recorded in `geo_data/regierungsviertel/mitte-garden-routes-v362-source.json.gz`. Footprint data uses © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. Imagery uses Geoportal Berlin, dl-de/zero-2-0. The viewer ships geometry and loads all additions from its own origin. No imagery is bundled.

Tests in `src/app/tests/mitte-garden-routes-v362.test.ts` cast vertical rays through the actual street and café groups in both styles. They check that all 35 individually identified bars are visible above the prior road geometry, mapped lawn panels and the crossing are open, paths meet the café floor, and an unexpected previous curb record is preserved. A second test restores the exact camera pose and projects three pixels where the desktop browser previously saw through the kerb. All three rays meet the paving at 5.265 m, while a neighbouring control ray still meets the road.
