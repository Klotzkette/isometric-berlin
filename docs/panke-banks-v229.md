# Lower Panke banks — v229

The lower Panke from the existing northern-city seam to the Nordhafen forebasin receives recessed bank faces along 4,901.444 m of retained mapped shoreline. The 1,182 segments come from the union of the existing OSM water polygons, so internal polygon joins do not become dams. Mapped tunnels are excluded; the Südpanke is not confused with the Nordhafen branch. No source water polygon, bridge, fish pass or upstream park is removed or widened.

The actual retained core curved-bank segments and their landward terrain samples are used inside the detailed city; this avoids partially buried brick faces. Outside it the existing urban display datum (ground 3 m, water −1.15 m) is preserved. `derive_panke_core_banks_v229.ts` records the exact retained source hashes and reproduces the existing bank curve/height functions offline. This is not a new hydrological survey. The central reach has estimated brick courses and restrained original abstract graffiti strokes, based on the user's observation. Material classification and individual marks are explicitly illustrative, not surveyed or copied artworks. The lower forebasin/Sellerpark reach is concrete; northern banks retain an earth/stone reading. Official descriptions document several construction types, including sheet-pile walls, and therefore do not justify applying brick to the entire river.

Sources: retained Geofabrik 2026-09-29 extract; [SenMVKU Panke works](https://www.berlin.de/sen/uvk/mobilitaet-und-verkehr/infrastruktur/wasserbau/ausbau-von-gewaessern/panke/) and [Panke measures](https://www.berlin.de/sen/uvk/umwelt/wasser-und-geologie/europaeische-wasserrahmenrichtlinie/berlin/panke/), checked 2026-10-18. The source receipt contains original water rings, centre lines, tunnel geometry and the retained GeoPackage hash.

The renderer uses compact source axes and independent 256 m culling cells, no textures, animation, lights or live map calls. Minecraft builds separate orthogonal bank and course blocks. Drawn mobile and desktop use identical static geometry. Constructor arrays are never stored on scene metadata.
