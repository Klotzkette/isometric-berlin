# Bebelplatz — the empty library

Pipeline step 10; bounded supplement to the retained OSM scene. Micha Ullman's
memorial uses exact node `262525336`, world `[1518.9904098202, 299.9489484141]`.
The original record remains canonical; its generic plaque geometry is replaced
by a flush glass aperture above a real three-dimensional white room.

The [bpb memorial entry](https://www.bpb.de/themen/holocaust/erinnerungsorte/503086/denkmal-buecherverbrennung/)
confirms the empty white shelves. Jörg Kuhn's entry,
[“Mahnmal Bücherverbrennung”, Bildhauerei in Berlin](https://bildhauerei-in-berlin.de/bildwerk/mahnmal-buecherverbrennung-5322/),
accessed 10 September 2026, specifies a 7.06 × 7.06 × 5.29 m room, fourteen shelf
levels and an imagined door in the north wall. Those explicit inventory values
are used instead of the eight-metre depth in Berlin.de's general tourist entry.
The [contemporary 1996 taz report](https://taz.de/Bebelplatz-Denkmal-Klaeglich/!1469294/)
states a 1.20 × 1.20 m pane. Its steel frame is flush with the paving; local shelf
depths, bay divisions, the paving bearing and frame thickness are procedural
presentation estimates, not an additional survey.

No books are added. Absence is the subject of the work. The two separate source
bronze plaques, nodes `12544932691` and `12544932692`, retain their existing
positions north and south of the glass; no duplicate plaques or quotations are
placed beside it.

Six coarse four-metre ground cells over the room are replaced by a thin paved
ceiling with a geometric opening. Continuous grey-beige paving follows the
exact retained OSM `sett` polygon (way `205728152`, 8299.218 m²), replacing the
orange appearance of the coarse `plazaBrick` display category. An eight-centimetre
display lift and depth offset keep it stable above the retained raster. It
preserves all surrounding cells and all canonical terrain arrays. The room has solid walls, empty shelves and a floor;
its window is real transparent geometry, not a picture. Source park/water/road
polygons do not cover this location. The visitor's existing terrain collision
continues across the closed glass surface, so this repair cannot make the
visitor fall into the room. Its internal white illumination remains legible at
night. Minecraft uses the same squared structural room and aperture with an
axis-aligned frame. Both versions have four renderables and 157 chamber
instances; no framebuffers, runtime images or per-frame updates
are added.

Photographs are external references only; neither original nor crop is bundled
or loaded. Focused ray tests check the empty opening, transparent pane, visible
chamber below and removal of exactly six raster cells without source mutation.

The v1.0.38 visibility pass adds a 16 cm deep pale aperture reveal, four small
flush frame-fastener cues and thin underside shading on the existing fourteen
empty shelf tiers. The pane tint is lighter and less opaque. These small local
display details keep the real 1.20 m aperture and sealed ground plane unchanged.
The shelves and floor are actual geometry, visible in inspected Chrome renders;
the narrow historical aperture naturally hides some upper shelf rows when viewed
from standing height. No artificial enlarged window, books or visitor staircase
is introduced.
