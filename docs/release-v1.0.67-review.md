# v1.0.67 — Oranienstraße and Oranienburger Straße

Pipeline step 10. Both requested corridors are refined within the existing
81.5 km² boundary; the 93-place tour, controls and viewing distance remain.

## Source-based detail

- [Neue Synagoge](neue-synagoge-v167.md): all four measured parts and 127 original
  wall/roof boundaries remain. The recessed three-arched front, gilded main
  dome, both smaller tower crowns and fine ribs receive separate detail.
  Missing upper-tower and crown dimensions are labelled visual estimates;
  the destroyed historical rear prayer hall is not reconstructed.
- [Tacheles](tacheles-v167.md): two measured parent buildings, current facade,
  stonework, large arched openings, courtyard and the open passage to Am
  Tacheles. Current free photographs contradict the source's 14 m pitched
  roof. The visible roof uses an explicitly estimated current silhouette;
  all 88 original boundary polygons and replacement decisions stay in evidence.
  Ground passage and the roof above it both have appropriate navigation.
- [Kinderbad Monbijou](monbijou-bath-v167.md): the exact asymmetric main basin
  and separate wading pool, light coping and orthophoto-checked paving.
  Existing changing buildings, paths, fences and trees remain unchanged.
  Surface offsets fit the shared terrain; documented pool depths do not
  imply newly surveyed/excavated terrain.
- [Oranien corridors](oranien-corridors-v167.md): 321 complete LoD2 parents,
  755 parts and 123 OSM street segments through Oranienstraße, Rio-Reiser-Platz,
  Mariannenstraße/Mariannenplatz and the short link to Görlitzer Bahnhof.
  The 125 earlier Oranienburger fronts keep their detail and receive shallow
  ornament. Facade subdivisions and untagged pavement dimensions are estimates.
  Café Jenseits is identified at Oranienstraße 16, separately from Bethanien.
  Its OSM business check is dated January 2025, not a fresh operating claim.
  Bethanien and St. Thomas retain their complete measured bodies and gain
  explicitly documented tower/drum supplements. The station's mapped canopy
  and two platforms are elevated instead of forming a false ground-level block.

The final integrator starts from the released v1.0.66 packets. Only 322 named
outer source owners and the generic facade contributions for prisms 19283679,
40754304 and 24054915 are replaced. The exact triangle/colour multisets of all
unowned geometry and all unowned navigation remain. Every old leaf footprint
has a refined source owner. Twelve packet pairs change; the full 273-descriptor
manifest is retained. The archived v166 audit is tested against its released
packets; a separate v167 audit checks the current packets directly against v166.

All four drawn modes share full pointer/touch detail. New global geometry uses
static batches and cancellable construction; Minecraft uses independent
orthogonal blocks. The native cumulative fixture records the new completed
world without overwriting any older fixture. There are no new runtime photos,
fonts or animations. Existing live packet/decode/GPU residency limits remain;
the finite offline package ceiling moves to 285 MiB for the 278.8 MiB built site.

Eleven inspected free photo references are appended to both attribution
manifests (463 records); all 452 earlier records remain unchanged and in order.
Berlin LoD2 and DOP are dl-de/zero-2-0, OSM is ODbL 1.0. No photographic pixels
or protected visitor plans are bundled. Exact credits are in the linked sources.
