## Runtime ownership and lifetime

- Required Moabit/West bodies are admitted against complete exact original
  prism receipts; native admission also binds complete affected voxel rows and
  exclusive five-value tuples. The unpublished factory transaction constructs
  and verifies the complete replacement before old geometry can yield, and
  disposes earlier allocations if a later constructor fails.
- The actual viewer enables the new families explicitly. Default factories
  stay closed. Initial, distant, progressive and Worker passes receive the
  admitted flags; the Worker imports ownership receipts rather than complete
  model factories. Navigation retains the old obstacle while the live admitted
  world is absent and selects independently represented drawn/native tops.
- Stream tickets capture all original mesh fingerprints, navigation and ink
  before older preparers. The final append requires those unchanged originals,
  the complete replacement fingerprint and valid indices. Retirement touches
  only the exact receipted triangle indices after complete append. Original
  packed body/roof buffers remain intact.
- Prepared navigation is closed by default. The two exact West StreetCurves
  road complements have explicit prepared-nav receipts. Post-admission origin,
  navigation, ink or append mutation revokes retirement. Copied public metadata
  and cloned private markers cannot grant admission.
- The West stream depends on its actually published complete core. Late
  admission/revocation re-decodes only affected existing receipts. Rechecks
  after fetch, decode and `onAttach` reject a stale result; unrelated residents
  remain attached. Existing mode revisions, abort/disposal and parking continue
  to own the packet lifetime.
- The 16-part, 400,000-vertex and 2,400,000-index packet limits remain enforced
  before and after older preparation. No residency, prefetch, decoder or
  descriptor limit was raised by this integration. Each finite recipe getter
  decodes its own plan and uses weak construction ownership where available;
  it does not inflate an entire region on an unrelated tile request.
