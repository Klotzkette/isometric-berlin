# Berlin universities and colleges, V322

This is a finite architectural correction within the named university campuses,
not a claim to have photogrammetrically surveyed every university property.
Published V1.0.201 source files, all earlier university architecture, source
roofs, courts, individual details and unrelated city geometry remain retained.

## Geometry and measured versus estimated information

The 17 measured new campus groups retain 483 complete official LoD2 leaves.
All original ground rings, holes and wall/roof sheets remain in
`geo_data/regierungsviertel/universities-v322-source.json.gz`, with exact
archive SHA-256 values and the mapped campus identities. The additional current
ASH group has two explicitly estimated vertical zones, giving 18 new groups
and 485 displayed parts in total. The official 39 m ASH tower height is retained;
the lower 18.8 m roof plane and internal height-zone boundary are display fits,
not invented official LoD2 measurements. The current mapped outline and open
courtyard are preserved completely.

Drawn source triangles use Float32 metre positions with original source normals,
without a new centimetre quantization of the surveyed sheets. One rigid vertical
datum applies to each complete parent. Existing Alt-Mitte datums are reused;
Holzlaube uses the adjoining retained FU mat datum. Other complete parents align
to the existing local display ground, explicitly not a new terrain survey.

Independent native construction samples the complete source surfaces on a
two-metre lattice and losslessly merges equal-colour occupied cells into
orthogonal runs. It is a separately constructed shell, not a smooth Drawn clone
or a hidden solid rectangle. Native windows are fitted to actual exposed
cardinal cell faces. Full Drawn and touch use identical static detail.

Window pitches, storey subdivisions, frames, mullions, sills, cedar joints and
colours are bounded reference-guided display estimates. They fit complete
actual wall polygons, including every corner and hole; they do not fill source
openings or claim measured individual apertures. Beuth keeps a brick reading
beside the pale Grashof technical grid. Holzlaube uses weathered grey cedar,
not yellow timber. Grimm's documented alternating narrow/wide 1.5 m architectural
rhythm belongs to its preserved existing body. The separate Hegelplatz families
use a regular concrete/glass facade fit; no Grimm rhythm is applied to them.

Twenty-one complete original Core owners and their twenty-one actual distant
previews yield through literal-pinned semantic recipes. Both actual Node/V8 and
Bun/JSC Drawn/preview constructors produced identical complete component words.
Actual full native city constructors ran sequentially, including required

staging and instance parking. All common native primitive/count/matrix/colour
words match between engines. The native catalogue retains all 4,278 relevant
original vertical column intervals; mixed columns with foreign contributors
remain unchanged. Shared obsolete columns have one actual matrix assignment,
while every contributing original owner is separately bound.

The existing government and university Core catalogues compose into one source
transaction per actual host. There is no early ID filter or second competing
matrix/index swap. A missing family, changed original word, foreign duplicate,
invalid replacement, authored resource disposal or broken family lifetime keeps
or restores the complete legacy source. Legitimate GPU/program retirement and
normal Worker evict/reload follow the existing private lifecycle contract.

The streamed correction uses 33 existing packets and 62 delivered coarse
campus owners. Raw XYZ/RGB words and source files are unchanged. Exact recorded
triangle, roof-ink and navigation indices yield only under the complete active
university model. Every other index, colour, line and navigation owner remains.
The compiler reuses the original V187 hero subtraction, protecting the adjoining
complete FU models. Exact alternative native roof diagonals are admitted only
as complete cardinal rectangles with identical four integer vertices and exact
covered area; the actual published triangles remain retained in evidence.

New floor coverage is only each verified named campus polygon minus the full
retained older scope. It is documented in
`universities-v322-scope-complement.json.gz`; no neighbouring district is filled.
Native new floors contain only whole cardinal cells inside that exact complement.
The current ASH courtyard uses the exact mapped site parcel `way/982919760`,
formerly marked as construction, beside its unchanged `way/1227439417` building
outline. The 2026 opening and completed architect site plan establish that this
is the finished named campus; the courtyard receives ground without a new body
or neighbouring-quarter expansion.
Walking follows source footprints, holes and actual roof triangles/native runs
through local spatial buckets. Points outside all campus buckets return before
checking the replacement lifetime. Tour stops, six modes, streaming/Worker,
decode, residency, prefetch and archive limits are unchanged.
