# Restaurant Paris–Moskau

The house at **Alt-Moabit 141** is represented as the small former railway
building recorded by the Berlin monument authority. The old generic source
roof and six coarse Native columns, plus the inaccurate authored full box,
are replaced only for the complete unchanged source identity
`DEBE01YYK0002Tlk` / compact `K0002Tlk` / OSM way `181479019`.

The corrected exterior has white plaster and dark blue-grey timber frames,
red brick window surrounds and quoins, three street windows and the distinct
door, two upper gable windows, a north-facing **open central loggia** with
individual rail posts, the projecting ornamental gable, two dormers,
chimneys and the photographed red east-side terrace awning. The continuous
glass/box proxy on the former south face is gone. The source footprint,
4.1m source floor and 14.7m principal roof peak remain measured facts;
the eave, timber, apertures and furnishing dimensions are explicitly
photo-estimated. A 20cm foundation continuation meets the retained 3.9m
front ground. No ground, route, interior or entrance navigation is invented.

`ParisMoskauV354.ts` admits a complete replacement only when the exact source
record matches; Native additionally requires all six old column tuples with
no duplicate. These columns have no other source owner at their sampled
centres. Each model attaches to the existing core world before the old owner

the Drawn geometry. The existing packed-root lifecycle and bounded lossless
