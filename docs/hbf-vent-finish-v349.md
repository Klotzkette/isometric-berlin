# Washingtonplatz ventilation tower finish — V349

The owner's clarification identifies the DB-logo ventilation tower on Washingtonplatz at Berlin Hauptbahnhof, separate from the BahnTower crown at Potsdamer Platz refined in V348.

## Evidence and precision

The [structural engineer's project record](https://www.sbp.de/projekt/berlin-hauptbahnhof-abluftkamin/) describes the 60 m tower, approximately 30 m² triangular section, three vertical wall planes mutually rotated 60°, steel frames in three fields, crossed tension rods and translucent glass-block infills. The engineer's [close-up photograph](https://www.sbp.de/app/uploads/2021/12/2156_1268_a_MAX.jpg) shows pale blue-white glass-block centres and light neutral joints within the existing dark steel frame. The [exterior photograph](https://www.sbp.de/app/uploads/2021/12/2156_1268_b_MAX.jpg) confirms that appearance at normal distance. No photograph is packaged or fetched by the viewer.

The existing `ArchitecturalLandmarks` station owner already supplies the 60 m height, triangular walls, three fields, braces, three DB badges, service plinth and antenna. The previous walls were opaque, uniformly grey sheets. V349 changes only their presentation to a subdued pale blue-grey frosted finish, faint neutral joints, shallow optical rims and slight block-to-block tone variation. The approximately 27.3 × 31.5 cm display module and optical treatment are **photo-informed estimates**, not surveyed glass-block dimensions.

## Exact boundary and preservation

Only the three factory-marked `Hauptbahnhof Washingtonplatz DB pylon` meshes qualify. Their dedicated shared material changes from grey to pale blue-grey; metallic response is reduced. Existing source profiles, positions, dimensions, vertex/index arrays, frames, logos, plinth, antenna, collision and movement remain unchanged. The opaque material and its depth rules remain: this is a frosted surface appearance, not a transparent wall or interior reconstruction.

The local-coordinate shader fades its small pattern using screen derivatives to avoid distant flicker. It adds no geometry, draw calls, textures, requests, timers, animation or shadow rebuilds. Scalar mesh provenance survives Worker serialization, and attachment-time rebinding restores the finish. Mode clones inherit only this finish while retaining earlier hooks. Actual disposal ends inheritance; bounded shader-program retirement does not. The independent Native station and palette remain unchanged. All desktop/mobile profiles retain their existing full geometry and budgets.

Focused validation checks material-only preservation, exact semantic admission, Worker-style metadata restoration, repeated binding, earlier shader hooks, mode clones, program retirement versus actual disposal, and standard/basic shader composition. Existing Hauptbahnhof tests continue to guard height, frames, logos, plinth and antenna. Release and browser validation belong to the root release report.
