# Gendarmenmarkt and Scheunenviertel facade depth — v1.0.83

Step 10 gives existing source-bound facades slightly stronger architectural
relief while preserving the complete earlier buildings and details. There are
no new tenant names, door openings, access permissions, textures, replacement
masses, navigation changes or packet rebuilds.

## Gendarmenmarkt

The established 16 facade families retain all 30 official source parents,
source frontages, local styles, signs, windows, balconies and roof geometry.
The existing builder receives one narrow recessed drip/shadow edge beneath each
window sill. The storefront routine receives a lower frame transom at each
already-authored bay. These fine sections make the current frames read more
clearly in unlit Day rendering, as well as Night and the other drawn modes.
The native factory receives its own box-based members through its established
separate builder path.

No facade bay or new architectural owner is inferred. Widths, section sizes and
the muted shadow tint are display estimates using the already documented
material/architecture evidence in
[the v146 source account](gendarmenmarkt-perimeter-sources-v146.md).
`GendarmenmarktPerimeterSource.json` is not regenerated by this pass.

The same 41 drawn / 32 native draw calls now contain **31,364 / 24,143
instances**, compared with the prior 28,799 / 21,578. All original v146 ceilings
are unchanged: less than 32,000 drawn / 25,000 native instances and 2.6 / 1.9 MB
GPU buffers. All nine existing source/frontage/courtyard/budget tests pass.

## Scheunenviertel

`build_scheunen_facades_v183.py` reads the committed
`geo_data/regierungsviertel/scheunenviertel-v168.json` offline and chooses at
most one longest eligible street wall per retained source parent. A wall must
be at least 8 m long and 10 m high, have its full frontage inside the existing
Scheunenviertel authoring polygon, face a retained OSM street within 22 m, and
have an unoccupied outward approach at 1, 3 and 5 m. Reserved detailed owners
remain excluded. No courtyard wall or shared party-wall seam is selected.

Exactly **97 current source walls** receive a shallow projecting eave cap,
undercut, low plinth and plinth lip, clipped to their original wall polygon.
Some sloping/incomplete source walls reject a member; the resulting total is
**370 cuboid members**. There are no new windows, signs or doors. All previous
v168 flat details, panes, shells, source holes, street packets and native
surfaces remain unchanged. Exact selected wall rings, parent and leaf-part IDs,
member ranges and the SHA-256 of the unchanged v168 source are committed in
`scheunen-facades-v183-evidence.json`.

The two photographs already credited in v168 were re-inspected at 960 px on
7 October 2026:

- [Almstadtstraße 25](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Berlin,_Mitte,_Almstadtstrasse_25,_Mietshaus.jpg),
  Jörg Zägel, CC BY-SA 3.0: shallow pale surrounds, dark sill undersides,
  projecting eave and restrained base courses.
- [Almstadtstraße 35](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Berlin,_Mitte,_Almstadtstrasse_35,_Mietshaus.jpg),
  Jörg Zägel, CC BY-SA 3.0: warm plaster, pale frames and sill/cornice relief.

These 2010 images inform a restrained neighbourhood material/profile reading;
they do not establish the exact present colour, restoration state or section
of every selected building. All profiles and colours remain explicitly
non-surveyed display estimates. The photos remain in the ignored raw cache,
and no new attribution record is needed because these files are already in the
shared manifests.

Lazy-load `ScheunenFacadesV183.createScheunenFacadesV183()` in drawn modes and
`MinecraftScheunenFacadesV183.createMinecraftScheunenFacadesV183()` in Minecraft.
Both use one final-count instance batch. The smooth JSON is **22,448 bytes**
and the native JSON is below **160 kB**. Native contains **2,262 independent
orthogonal contour steps** on the same selected walls; it does not import the
smooth payload. Pointer and touch receive identical drawn detail. Both factories
can share the compact, data-free `justicePalaceV183Batches` helper. No owner
mask or suppression change is required.
