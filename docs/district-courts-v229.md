# Current Berlin district courts — Step 10 / v229

The current official justice/service directory identifies **eleven Amtsgerichte**. This supplement treats their **twenty current service-building groups separately**, plus the specifically requested northern Landgericht Berlin II annex at Tegeler Weg. The latter is not an Amtsgericht. There is no district-wide fill and no former Kantstraße courthouse or former Pankow-Weißensee institutional name.

## Geometry and preservation

`district-courts-source-v229.json` retains the named OSM records and complete cached official LoD2 leaf sheets where available. Every original wall/roof surface remains in the new complete shells; rigid display-ground offsets preserve the metric architecture. Missing complete archives use retained official outlines or exact OSM footprints, and are explicitly marked. Storey/height defaults and portal, pane, reveal and material proportions are illustrative estimates, not facade surveys. No undocumented tower is invented.

Wedding's twelve official parts contain roof elevations from 25.71 to 34.33 m before the rigid display offset. Static facing tones make those existing slopes legible in the unlit drawn renderer, without adding vertices or lighting work. Charlottenburg's retained official object contains a single horizontal roof surface at 26.49 m before its offset; that source limitation remains explicit rather than inventing a pitched roof.

Wedding substitutes only original core owner `-6874671`, after full original-row equality and exact native-column receipts. Charlottenburg owner `DEBE04YY50000NDH` and Tegeler-Weg north annex owner `DEBE04YY500004wz` substitute only independently replayed original coloured triangles, ink and collision rows in both mode families. Changed packets or changed prism fields retain the old geometry conservatively. The v166/v183 Moabit and Littenstraße models and the old Tegeler-Weg Landgericht remain complete.

Previously absent AG Köpenick, its separately mapped Grundbuch building and AG Lichtenberg get only their finite named envelope and a one-metre foundation apron. `bounds-district-courts-v229.geojson` records that exact scope in WGS84. No unrelated streets, houses or landscape are added. All other locations retain their delivered source envelopes and window grids; only shallow corners, ledges, portals and service lettering are added. Shared police/OVG/VG premises receive only small service plaques.

## Runtime and integration

- `createDistrictCourtsV229(native=false)` — 21 independent static site groups with complete drawn mobile detail and separate orthogonal native members.
- `createDistrictCourtEnvelopesV229(native=false)` — 6 required site shells, including all three exact-owner replacements.
- `districtCourtsV229RoofAt`, `TerrainAt`, `GroundAt`, `SolidAt` — complete shell collision/roof support, native block tops included; feet-gated support prevents upward teleportation.
- Construction-only JSON `sites` fields: `districtCourtsV229.json`, `districtCourtsV229Envelopes.json`, `districtCourtsV229Native.json`. The compact navigation data and exact receipts remain available for their runtime purpose.
- `districtCourtCorePreviousV229.ts` guards drawn/distant/native/collision transfer. `districtCourtPacketNavigationV229.ts` and existing `exactPacketTransfer` guard surrounding packets. Preview restore and parked buffers keep the transferred state.
- No residency budget, dynamic frame work, per-frame allocations, textures, shadows, tour count or mobile quality setting is changed. Static transforms and exact-sized GPU buffers are frozen.

`district-courts-v229-cameras.json` contains all 21 audit views. `district-courts-identities-v229.json` contains current official location/architecture references; no photograph is bundled. Source geometry licences remain Berlin dl-de/zero-2-0 and OpenStreetMap ODbL.
