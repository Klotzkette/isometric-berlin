# The outer stone field at the Sinti and Roma Memorial

The existing model preserves the 12 m reflecting basin and its 237 surrounding granite slabs, including 69 named stones. Intersections with all surveyed and raster ground triangles showed that all 129.44 m² of those slabs were already exposed. The pale corners in the earlier overview came from coarse ground around the separate pedestrian apron. The old model's inner stone shapes remain explicitly estimated.

The new exterior follows the registered OSM apron and the actual individual granite outlines visible in the official TrueDOP 2024 image. This flight shows the stones, grassy joints and mineral access with comparatively little tree shadow. The TrueDOP 2026 view was inspected for comparison. Its imagery does not enter the generator. The frozen 2024 source image is a 700 × 700 pixel WMS sample of EPSG:25833 bounds 389790, 5819798, 389825, 5819833. Its sampling interval is 5 cm. Orthophoto tracing has image-scale uncertainty and does not identify individual inscriptions. [Berlin TrueDOP 2024](https://gdi.berlin.de/services/wms/truedop_2024?SERVICE=WMS&REQUEST=GetCapabilities) · [OpenStreetMap relation 8329726](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/8329726)

The photographed outer field occupies 176.957 m² beyond the old 9.55 m construction radius. The attributed ground comprises 74.080 m² of grass and 102.877 m² of mineral substrate. The brighter outer granite outlines occupy 63.389 m² over that ground. The granite projects 4 cm above the prior local ground as a display estimate. The five material classes share one indexed draw and preserve the 12 m basin, all 237 original fitted slabs and the old grass joints. None of the 513 surveyed tree-ground contacts intersects this exterior. A complete second comparison after the adjacent woodland change adjusted ten tree heights and confirmed the other 503 within 1 mm.

The frozen packet was prepared with GEOS 3.14.1, Shapely 2.2.0, NumPy 2.3.5, CPython 3.12.14 and zlib 1.3.2. Its SHA-256 is `2fa46b7aa978e07c0b5d38e166b49ca44896f70f6c7cba8b77a7cfa2fd45c228`. GEOS 3.13.1 also reproduces every one of the 5,348 millimetre XYZ/material vertices and the same 5,472 oriented triangles' surface coverage. It uses the other diagonal for two triangles of a single horizontal mineral quadrilateral. The regression compares material groups and oriented planes in integer millimetres and checks the exact packet on the recorded toolchain.

The generator combines the frozen orthophoto, the OSM polygon and its water hole, and world-coordinate triangles of the local ground, paths and lawns. Run `python scripts/build_sinti_roma_apron_v375.py` to reproduce the source packet. The Geoportal Berlin data use Datenlizenz Deutschland – Zero – Version 2.0.
