# Kienberg and the IGA cableway

The cableway connects the Gärten der Welt station at Blumberger Damm, the Wolkenhain station on the Kienberg and the Kienbergpark station near the U5 entrance. The viewer follows both mapped OpenStreetMap sections and preserves the three surveyed station footprints. The end stations have green roofs and the middle station has open covered waiting areas. The model uses the full official roof sheets, with separate block geometry in Native. The station heights and roofs come from 13 complete LoD2 building leaves. The support members and glass colours are visual estimates. [Grün Berlin station plan](https://gruen-berlin.de/projekte/infrastruktur/seilbahn/linie-stationen) · [Station architects](https://krp-architektur.com/iga-3/) · [Station engineering sheets](https://eisat.de/fileadmin/images/projekte/IGA17/IGA17.pdf) · [OpenStreetMap west section](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/654590808) · [OpenStreetMap east section](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/335201546)

The two sides of the continuous rope carry 64 illustrative static cabins. Nine individual support positions are mapped, including supports near the stations. The operator counts six supports outside station areas and the manufacturer counts 13 overall. The model follows the nine mapped positions. Local terrain and surface-model samples help establish the display heights. Rope sag, cabin spacing and small dimensions are estimates. The static arrangement shows the installed fleet in a representative operating layout. [Operator's technical specification](https://gruen-berlin.de/projekte/infrastruktur/seilbahn/seilbahntyp) · [LEITNER technical reference and photographs](https://www.leitner.com/unternehmen/referenzen/detail/gd10-seilbahn-gaerten-der-welt/)

The measured Wolkenhain roof and parapet describe an elevated, irregular ring with an open centre. The source includes simplified walls that run from the rim all the way down to the foundation. Photographs and the architect's description show an open structure under the platform. The model represents this with the white faceted membrane, slim branching supports, stairs and a lift. The café retains its complete measured source envelope. The 89.47 m source rim in the viewer datum is approximately 119.47 m NHN. The operator gives the viewpoint height as approximately 120 m. The individual strut, stair and lift details are visual estimates. [Kienbergpark Wolkenhain photographs](https://www.kienbergpark.de/entdecken-erleben/wolkenhain/) · [Wolkenhain architects](https://krp-architektur.com/iga-2/) · [Steelwork contractor's project images](https://stahlbau.vollack.de/projekte/wolkenhain)

The operator’s park plan records the relationship between the summit, stations and park entrances. Terrain is tied to the official Berlin height datum. The source file retains the surveyed geometry, public references and raw input hashes for 11 exact LoD2 families and all individually mapped rope anchors. Photo and orthophoto pixels remain outside the viewer.
