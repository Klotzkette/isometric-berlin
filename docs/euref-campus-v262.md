## Recognizable forms and source limits

The current Gasometer includes the green lower mantle, glazed office bands, a terrace edge at approximately 66 m above local ground, and the visible open steel frame above it. The operator describes the January 2024 completed office building and a roughly one-metre separation from the historic frame. The frame is 78 m high in the operator's detailed description, rounded to 80 m on its general campus page. The professional historic description supplies **24 posts, seven horizontal rings and six braced levels**. Equal 13 m ring spacing, brace widths, collars, colours, window divisions and facade offsets are explicitly unsurveyed display estimates. No interior plan, lettering, engineering cross-section or exact office-storey count is claimed.

**The Gasometer plan sources conflict.** The preserved LoD2 cylinder is approximately 60.8 m in diameter and 65.484 m high. Its footprint follows the historic outer OSM ring, approximately 60.7 m across. The OSM new inner body, way **1307229197**, is approximately 47.8 m across and has only about 62% of that area. Complete containment of the smaller footprint does not establish identity. The architect/operator's one-metre clearance supports a much larger inner diameter than that OSM outline. Both OSM rings, their areas and this conflict remain in the frozen source. The full existing cylinder is kept; the visible glazing follows that retained envelope, and the **31.5 m frame radius is a declared display reconciliation**, not a surveyed current glass diameter. No source is silently discarded to make the references agree.

The native frame uses a **33.5 m radius**, an explicit 2 m grid clearance around the independently stepped old cylinder; native facade fittings also stand farther out to clear its coarse cells. Its steel braces, rings, curved glass and all campus facade fittings are independent axis-aligned blocks. The drawn version uses thin oriented boxes. Both versions keep the same source identity, feature inventory and full static touch detail; neither introduces textures, DOM/canvas drawing, fonts, animation or a new per-frame loop.

Eight historic owners transfer from exact old flat prisms to **all 15 measured parts / 430 source polygons**, including the Wasserturm's stepped roof and low wings, Retortenhaus, Schmiede and annexes, Schleusenhaus, Reglerhaus, Haus 3 and Werkstatt 26. Rendering uses every measured wall and roof sheet; ground sheets remain in the complete frozen source. Facade windows, industrial transoms and sills are fitted within measured exterior wall polygons. Haus 14's curved ribbons follow its retained full polygon perimeter. Modern office bands remain additive to their older shells.

**Modern roof-height limits remain explicit.** In Haus 23–24 the older retained coarse height is 40.31 m while the extracted measured leaves reach 36.685 m above the chosen ground; Haus 10–11 gives 34.50 m versus 33.982 m. The new source-bound window planes can therefore stop below the retained older roof. These shells were not transferred, lowered or described as newly corrected measured roofs. Some OSM/LoD2 plan matches are partial, especially Schleusenhaus and the Schmiede annex; the source stores intersection and both footprint areas, not a false exact-match assertion.

## Complete preservation and finite delivery

Only the existing descriptors `-3_7`, `-3_8` and `-2_7` point to six new `euref262-` assets. There is **no new descriptor, footprint, protected campus rectangle or catalogue extension**. The old public assets remain byte for byte. Every old encoded mesh part, index, line and navigation record also remains unchanged inside the new packets; the complete measured geometry is appended. The generator independently reproduces the eight old prisms and matches every intended retirement at the existing centimetre precision, without a fuzzy or area-equivalent exception.

`eurefTransfersV262` checks complete original mesh/line/navigation fingerprints and every appended replacement part before marking an atomic runtime transfer. Older v233 roofs/doors/curbs and v254 material receipts therefore receive exactly their original source. Only after those preparations and material passes do the final decoded buffers collapse the explicitly receipted old EUREF triangles and roof-ink segments. Navigation then substitutes the complete measured parts; all unrelated navigation remains unchanged. The EUREF historical owners have no overlap with the older v233 roof/door owners. Missing or modified replacement geometry fails closed and cannot retire a source body.

The six receipts retire 977 drawn and 1,421 native old triangles and 331 drawn ink segments. Complete new part ownership/navigation covers all 15 parts in each mode. Old and new asset bytes, decoded bytes, exact runtime indices, retained mesh/line complements and old owned navigation are recorded in `euref-v262-evidence.json`. The six compressed files total **1,951,063 bytes**, each below 650 kB compressed / 2.6 MB decoded. The compact static supplement uses 15,278 drawn or 24,666 native instances, one batch per mode, below **2.1 MB** of geometry/instance buffers per mode. Existing live resident, packet, queue and camera-tour limits are unchanged.

`eurefV262GroundAt` supplies only the visible shallow terrace-edge support. The existing source roofs/ground remain authoritative elsewhere. `eurefV262SolidAt` follows individual posts, horizontal/diagonal members and the terrace parapet. The upper frame's central space remains open; there is no new 78 m solid cylinder. Native member collision follows the independent block dimensions. Thin facade fittings do not invent interiors or new campus routes.

The normal generator uses only the compact frozen source and exact predecessor assets. `--extract` is the explicit optional source refresh from the one retained official archive and the bounded retained OSM response; it does not scan the whole city. It rejects every unrelated metadata/order/coverage change. The v261 predecessor adapter invokes it before its own v261 check, preserving the older release lineage.
