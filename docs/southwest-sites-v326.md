# Southwest sites V326 — release 207

This finite addition covers the complete mapped Pfaueninsel and the adjacent
Brücke-Museum/Kunsthaus Dahlem pair. It preserves all earlier V187/V190/V194
source, trees, lake courses, paths, measured surfaces and correct authored detail.
It does not fill neighboring districts. `southwest-sites-v326-source.json.gz`
retains the complete original island geometry, all 251 local polygon records,
69 mapped path courses, original source tags, complete measured family sheets,
archive identities and SHA-256 pins for the six unchanged older inputs.

The entire island path network is triangulated inside the mapped shoreline,
subtracting actual mapped water and building footprints. Unknown path widths use
an explicit illustrative two metres. Additional interior woodland trees occupy
only mapped wood/forest outside the earlier V187 domain; their complete crowns
clear paths, buildings and water. Their individual positions are illustrative,
not a tree survey. Rose shrubs and open aviary wire cues retain their actual
mapped garden/building identities. Existing exterior woodland remains untouched.

Schloss Pfaueninsel retains its complete earlier body and 99 earlier fittings.
The additive layer gives its white timber cladding fine board joints, tower
window trim and an open iron bridge lattice. It uses the restored 2025 appearance
as reference; temporary restoration scaffolding is not reproduced. Brücke-Museum
retains every earlier measured wall/roof and 270 fittings. New small concrete
tie-hole cues occupy its long source walls; no invented glazing closes the
deliberately blank concrete elevations. Its identity and architecture remain
distinct from the neighboring former studio.

Kunsthaus Dahlem is the former Breker studio at Käuzchensteig 8/10/12, not an
additional wing of Brücke-Museum. Its three complete measured families have six
leaves. The main source roof rises to 31.356m in the existing local terrain frame,
where the old flat OSM fallback stopped at 25.66m. The new model keeps the source
roof slopes, full studio wings, annexes and complete wall sheets. Muted yellow
brick, tall studio glazing and restrained joint courses are source-plane-bound
recognition fittings; they are not a measured individual window inventory.

Four additional complete measured island buildings retain their original
unequal masses and roof courses: Kavalierhaus (four leaves), Meierei (four),
Maschinenhaus (four), and Luisentempel (one). The latter is the rectangular
four-column Doric portico with a rear wall and open sides. Its closed LoD2 box is
retained as evidence but not rendered as a closed room: the actual roof and
pediment remain, four open columns support it, and navigation stays open below
the roof except at those actual columns/rear wall. No circular rotunda is added.
Unmeasured window spacing on the other heritage buildings remains illustrative.

Exactly five old OSM fallback owners can yield: `OSM-way-32212224` and
`OSM-way-112235457` (Kunsthaus), `OSM-way-73427143` (Meierei), and
`OSM-way-25015199` plus `OSM-way-1545433869` (Maschinenhaus and its actual source
building part). Complete raw and prepared packet receipts bind every mesh,
source origin, line and navigation record. Nine retire-only SourceQuarter
recipes retire exactly 569 old triangles and 66 old roof-line segments. Native
ownership is checked against every actual neighboring navigation contributor;
no positive-area foreign owner shares the retired cells. All unselected indices
and original packet files remain byte-identical. Complete measured roofs/Nav
replace the old proxies only after the new privately complete model is actually
attached to its world. The retirement gate is closed by default.

Native rendering is an independent cardinal exterior shell on a fixed half-metre
grid, with separately stepped architectural fittings. It does not rotate Drawn
meshes or fill hidden interiors. Its actual shell columns provide matching roof
caps/navigation. The island path surface is separately cardinal. Drawn keeps
the same complete static detail on touch. No renderer, decode, vertex/index,
residency, startup, Worker or prefetch limit is increased.

Six illustrative freely roaming peacocks include four blue and two white birds.
This is an illustrative visible population, not a claim about the actual number
on the island. Every complete route has 0.9m of mapped-path clearance and 2.5m of
land/water/building clearance. The existing shared frame clock drives the two
representations at most ten times per second; no RAF, timer, texture or shadow
atlas update is added. Hidden ancestors, offscreen/distant cameras, hidden pages
and reduced motion pause it without catch-up. Family changes retain the shared
clock. Drawn and Native respectively own 481,680B and 98,840B of bounded geometry
and animation buffers, below the 650,000B allowance. Teardown and ordinary
resource disposal close the update callback; qualified GPU/program retirement
does not destroy the authored animation.

## Integration

`createSouthWestSitesV326Steps(native,onCreate?)` is cooperative; the synchronous
`createSouthWestSitesV326(native)` drains the same factory. The private raw
`hasCompleteSouthWestSitesV326(root,native?)` and actual attachment gate
`hasSouthWestSitesRuntimeV326(world,root,native)` are separate.
`disposeSouthWestSitesV326(root)` is idempotent. Hidden mode ancestors are valid;
detached roots, changed words/membership/material shapes/poses or authored
disposal close admission. Legitimate static interleave, lossless parking,
Day/Night/Schwellenraum/Native presentation and program retirement remain valid.

Use SourceQuarter kind `southwest-sites-v326`, its new
`southWestSitesV326Packets.json`, and `requiredReplacement:true`. Check the actual
private world gate before and after each async attachment and decode checkpoint.
There is no Government/Core/Worker ownership transaction for these sites.
Navigation transfers reside inside the same finite packet recipes.

Create `createPfaueninselPeacockClockV326()` once outside an Outline family
rebuild, then `createPfaueninselPeacocksV326(native,clock)`. Call its
`userData.update(timestamp,camera,reducedMotion)` in the existing frame loop and
`userData.dispose()` before dropping the family. Do not static-park its dynamic
position/instance buffers or introduce a separate animation scheduler.
