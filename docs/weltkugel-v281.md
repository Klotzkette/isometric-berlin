# Weltkugelbrunnen v281 — Step 10

The 1983 Joachim Schmettau fountain on Breitscheidplatz now has a west-facing
semicircular granite basin, segmented hemispherical granite globe, east-side
spill steps, a bronze ark and small figure/animal recognition, with static water
arcs. It replaces the earlier square plate and simple dome. This is an authored
recognition model; the precise globe, figure and water dimensions are photo-guided
display estimates, not a measured sculpture survey.

## Evidence and limits

- [Bezirksamt Charlottenburg-Wilmersdorf](https://www.berlin.de/ba-charlottenburg-wilmersdorf/ueber-den-bezirk/bauwerke/brunnen/artikel.118259.php): artist, 1983, red granite/bronze, figurative motifs and relation to the lower Europa-Center courtyard.
- [Landesdenkmalamt, 09040105](https://denkmaldatenbank.berlin.de/daobj.php?obj_dok_nr=09040105): the protected ensemble includes fountain, stairs, café and shops, 1981–83.
- [Gedenktafeln in Berlin](https://www.gedenktafeln-in-berlin.de/gedenktafeln/detail/weltkugelbrunnen): west-facing semicircular basin, Ark and hemispherical center, curved east cascade and figurative details.
- Official reference photographs viewed on 26 October 2026: `wasserklops.jpg`, © BACW/Farchmin, and `mdb-060822_021.jpg`, © BACW/KHMM, 22 August 2006. Reference only; no photo pixels or textures are distributed.
- Retained OpenStreetMap way 120866116 supplies the full 26-vertex footprint, © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. Its original 9 m prism height was an inappropriate generic fallback, not a measured fountain height.

The sunken café/shop courtyard and underground stair arrangement cannot be
reconstructed reliably from the retained surface data. They are not invented or
claimed complete. The shallow upper spill steps in this model are fountain
fittings, not a substituted route to the underground court. The nearby independent
Marionettenbrunnen is outside this replacement.

## Exact ownership and preservation

`build_weltkugel_ownership_v281.py` retains and fingerprints the entire old
26-point source ring, height/base, class, roof and tone. Only that exact false
9 m prism (ID 20866116) yields in the drawn core. Native replacement retires
exactly 17 complete exclusive columns with their original base/top/kind tuples.
A changed owner or overlap fails closed. All original source files remain
unchanged and the complete mapped footprint is visible in the new low granite
base. No bounding-box deletion or neighbouring structure is involved.

The old navigation prism yields to the same exact source guard. The actual
upper globe is a physical obstacle; water arcs and illustrative figures are not
large invented collision boxes. The lower courtyard remains on the existing
terrain pending better evidence.

The new drawn fountain is attached in the required site transaction, before
world publication; the later culture-detail pass omits that exact fountain to
avoid duplication. Native construction is already part of its required world.
The new drawn fountain is one separate batched mesh. Keeping it separate avoids
pushing the existing church mesh over the 16-bit index boundary and preserves
its original geometry/budget. A frozen `legacy` recipe keeps the prior ensemble
hash test; all surrounding church, benches, plaza and lighting bytes compare
unchanged against the current model with only the named fountain group removed.
Full and mobile drawn construction are identical.

The Minecraft interpretation is independent cardinal surface cells and cuboids,
including joined stepped hemisphere skins; it does not rotate a drawn model or
fill the hemisphere with invisible voxels. It adds no texture or animation and
does not alter runtime residency, decoder, package or tour limits.

Focused tests cover exact/changed ownership, all source ring vertices, actual
water/globe ray intersections, full/mobile identity, cardinal finite native
geometry, unchanged neighbouring model and globe-versus-water navigation.

Independent review found hidden bronze panels and lower diamond strokes. Their
mountings now follow the actual drawn sphere and independent native surface.
470 real colour-aware ray intersections cover all three panels and both diamond
reliefs in both modes. The final fountain uses 201,846 drawn buffer bytes or
166,936 native bytes (2,188 instances), within the unchanged CityWest limits.
The full/mobile comparison includes native instance matrices and colours.
