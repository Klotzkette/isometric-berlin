# DomAquarée and Littenstraße recognition — v288

`BerlinHistoricCoreV288.ts` constructs the complete measured DomAquarée hotel,
eastern mixed-use block and northwest office. All 32 original parts, 518 source
sheets, vertices, holes and frozen viewer elevations remain. Six exact old
coarse records may yield only after the complete factory and both source guards
pass: `02156993`, `12706358`, `12706359`, `24351835`, `24351837`, `70855133`.
Eight original-proxy digitization slivers outside the official footprint remain
as separate complete skins, totalling 84.099989315 m². The hotel binding's seven
other source families are outside this transfer and must remain. There is no
whole-binding exclusion.

The native receipt contains 576 canonical columns. Every full 4 m cell has zero
positive-area overlap with the five foreign neighbouring core footprints;
matching centres alone were insufficient for eleven cells in the initial
three-owner candidate. The full bounded native inventory contains 667 tuples.
Missing, changed, duplicate or foreign records preserve the original family.
Getters, symbols, sparse coordinate arrays and unexpected owner keys fail closed.
The small ownership module imports no construction geometry. Factory admission
checks private provenance, current child identities, actual buffers, counts,
materials and transforms. Navigation has its own small rings/holes/roof-plane
profile and captures no construction parts or wall sheets.

The source already contains the courtyards; they were never solid rectangles.
The correction restores actual source heights and sloping/curved roof sheets.
High roof glazing tones, source-edge steel sections, bay divisions and material
tones are reference-guided estimates. Material classification does not change
any source surface geometry. The independent native envelope uses 2 m world-axis
surface cells, roof-aware steps, open source courts and exterior glazing. It is
not a rotated smooth model or a filled interior.

Additional rows append after all existing justice-family members. They add
divided glazing and French rails at Klostergärten, facade fittings at the active
B&B Hotel Voltairestraße 3a, and VoltAir's pale vertical glass frit and dark glazed

is a material cue, not a carved void. Existing roofs, source cubes, courts and
earlier detail remain. Native fittings are projected onto actual exterior
rectangles from the delivered `5_0`/`6_0` native packets. Their full member bounds
remain within the supporting rectangles and at least 0.02 m outside the surface.

The Litten portal receives small joints on its existing v257 arches; its giant
order, existing windows, curved crown and source contour remain. Native joints
clad actual v257 block faces. No demolished Grunerstraße towers are rebuilt.
The Klosterruine LoD2 is not a complete survey of the standing ruin: it labels
the northern aisle's high fragments as sloping roof sheets, while the nave and
choir are a low 5.803 m cap. All 116 sheets remain unchanged. The low cap remains

apertures, rather than dark opaque window panels. The southern nave stays open;
no destroyed southern arcade, church roof, spire or nineteenth-century towers
are reconstructed. Wall heights (about 15–19 m above the retained floor), small
insets, arch shapes, tracery and subdivisions are explicit photo-guided estimates,
not new measured source heights.

The independent native reading uses cardinal masonry runs on the actual 2 m
source floor at Y7. Every complete new member AABB clears every touched frozen
