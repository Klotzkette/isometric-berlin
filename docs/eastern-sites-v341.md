# Eastern sites and Europacity: fine detail v341

This is a bounded continuation at existing admitted buildings, not a new survey
of the entirety of Alexanderplatz, Karl-Marx-Straße or Karl-Marx-Allee. All
original source bodies, roofs, openings, routes, private model receipts and
complete older fittings remain. The new members are small surface fittings.
Their dimensions, spacing and material colours are photograph-informed display
estimates; no newly measured dimensions are claimed.

## Alexanderplatz

- CUBIX: staggered vertical stone joints complement the existing horizontal
  joints on the exact retained facade planes. They sit behind its projecting
  glazed volumes. The reference is the architect's [Tchoban Voss project
  page](https://tchobanvoss.de/de/projects/cubix-am-alexanderplatz), photograph
  © Klemens Renner.
- RathausPassagen: thin lower window transoms and upper lips use the positions
  and dimensions of the existing residential-window recipe. The supporting
  close view is the owner's [WBM RathausPassagen page](https://www.wbm.de/gewerbe-berlin/rathauspassagen/).

The new conceptual members are 876 stone joints and 2,280 residential window
members. Drawn totals become 8,359 boxes and 296 strokes. Independently cardinal
Native totals become 32,406 boxes. The original 5,203/296 Drawn and 24,690 Native
members are checked as exact prefixes, including coordinates and colours.

## Karl-Marx-Allee

- Kino International: 224 shallow nonfigurative folded lozenge fields articulate
  the border rows of its fourteen existing relief panels. Original figure
  impressions are retained. The photographed geometric background is documented
  by [Bezirksamt Mitte's funded refurbishment project](https://www.kma-mitte.de/projekte/sanierung-des-kinos-international).
  The original artwork is by Waldemar Grzimek, Karl-Heinz Schamal and Hubert
  Schiefelbein, as credited in the [Berlin heritage history walk](https://www.berlin.de/ost-west-ost-kulturbahnhoefe/history-walk/artikel.1558584.php).
  These original model impressions do not trace the artwork or distribute its
  photograph. The [operator's completed 2026 refurbishment record](https://presse.yorck.de/assets/2026_International_Fakten.pdf)
  takes precedence over an older project-page construction status.
- Café Moskau: two narrow rails per selected roof edge and 22 short stanchions
  sit behind the retained roof lettering. The reference is the
  [Landesdenkmalamt restoration record](https://www.berlin.de/landesdenkmalamt/denkmale/aus-der-praxis-erkennen-und-erhalten/oeffentliche-anlagen/das-restaurant-moskau-641117.php)
  and the [Berlin history walk](https://www.berlin.de/ost-west-ost-kulturbahnhoefe/history-walk/artikel.1558588.php).
  LDA photographs retain their original credit, including Wolfgang Bittner.

Drawn cinema totals become 622 boxes, 2,231 strokes and one sphere; Native totals
become 13,743 cardinal boxes. The original 596/887/1 and 8,675/0/0 outputs are
verified as exact prefixes. No original relief, glazed foyer, open atrium,
Sputnik, signage or replacement/navigation receipt is removed or changed.

## Karl-Marx-Straße, Neukölln

The [district's Rathaus record](https://www.berlin.de/ba-neukoelln/ueber-den-bezirk/sehenswertes/artikel.105690.php)
and [Berlin monument record 09090494](https://denkmaldatenbank.berlin.de/daobj.php?obj_dok_nr=09090494)
support the town hall's historic facade and tower. Its existing 2019/official
reference photographs already retained for v305 show subdivided opening lights
and small openings in the upper tower lantern. The new recipe adds two thin
crossbars to each eligible old pane, plus twelve upper lantern windows.
Native lantern windows sit outside the actual union of the retained cardinal
lantern blocks; they are not rotated copies of the smooth facade.

The Arcaden's fine metal/glass transoms follow the existing panes and the
[project engineer's photograph](https://www.tga-effizienz.de/details-dienstleistungen/berlin-neuk%C3%B6lln-arcaden-mit-cineplex-kino.html).
The [current operator](https://www.neukoelln-arcaden.de/) identifies the building;
historical image tenants are not introduced as current signage.

Added rows: Drawn 4,754 (3,488 Rathaus bars, 1,254 Arcaden bars, 12 lantern
windows); Native 4,674 (3,482, 1,180, 12). The full v305 source arrays are never
mutated, and the complete model's private receipt includes the extra fittings.
They use its existing opaque batch and do not alter navigation solids.

## Europacity

The opt-in `fineReliefV341` option is enabled in both runtime world builders.
The default constructor remains unchanged for older explicit callers.

- KPMG / Heidestraße 58: shallow limestone sill lips under admitted existing
  panes emphasize the natural-stone relief described by the architects at
  [KSP Engel](https://www.ksp-engel.com/projekte/kpmg-headquarters-berlin).
  The reference image is © Adrian Schulz. Existing floor placement and source
  envelopes remain authoritative; there is no new surveyed sill profile.
- QH Track: slender vertical flutes on existing green spandrel fields of the
  five taller tower profiles. The nine-part ensemble is documented by
  [EM2N](https://www.em2n.ch/en/work/quartier-heidestrasse-qh-track.html).
  The [actual facade contractor's project photographs](https://www.hemmerlein-sichtbeton.de/referenzen/qh-track-berlin-01102023/)
  show vertically corrugated green infills and concrete surrounds. Photograph
  © Linus Lintner. Four drawn ribs per retained panel and three per admitted
  native cell are an economical material impression, not a measured rib count.

With the full retained source context, Drawn adds 9,191 members (763 limestone
lips and 8,428 green flutes); full Native adds 5,292 (426 and 4,866). They occupy
the existing two material batches, adding no draw calls. The Native additions
are constructed independently on exposed cardinal faces of the actual 4 m
source cells, restricted to cells overlapping the corresponding source owner.
Adjacent occupied cells reject an attachment. Only identity and storey elevation
are shared with the existing native facade recipe. Drawn mobile and desktop
remain identical. Original native mobile density is not reduced.

## Preservation and validation

No image is shipped, and there is no new fetch, external asset, animation,
coverage descriptor, source replacement or navigation obstruction. All six
styles keep the same existing batches and material bindings. The browser must
still be checked separately; the tests below are geometry/preservation checks,
not a claim of visual browser review.

Executed successfully:

- 23 tests: `eastern-fine-detail-v341`, `alexander-recognition-v257`,
  `east-cinemas-v260`, `neukoelln-landmarks-v305`.
- 7 tests: `europacity-fine-detail-v341`, `europacity-architecture`.

The preservation tests hash complete pre-v341 construction prefixes, retain
all old source arrays, verify cardinal native additions outside real solid
faces, check exposed source-cell support, and exercise existing source-owner
admission, six-mode binding, parking and navigation guards. The v305 test's
actual runtime-hook fixture now supplies the v340 front modules already used
by that hook; no production guard is bypassed.

All linked external photos remain reference-only under their publishers' and
photographers' rights. No new photo licences or third-party artwork reuse rights
are asserted.
