## Visible improvement and preservation

The twenty-four selected ordinary source frontages already carry **288 drawn and 82 native v188 pane rectangles**. This pass preserves every one of those panes and their existing rhythm. It adds depth around **287 existing drawn panes** through two narrow side members, a lintel and a projecting sill. It adds the same four-member treatment around **36 eligible existing native panes**, each anchored independently to its own cardinal native wall and retained pane. No new upper-floor window grid or duplicate glazing is created.

The numbers describe new eligible treatments, not reductions of the old model. Forty-six native panes do not have the required complete delivered ground strip at the same source floor and receive no extra frame. One drawn candidate is too close to a mapped entrance. Their existing panes and walls remain unchanged. Packet `9_4` has a deliberate empty native addition; it retains the complete native source.

Four mapped commercial-use points add modest lower shopfront fittings: one each on owners `DEBE02YY400002CF` and `DEBE02YY400002IP`, and two on `DEBE02YY40000DWS`. All are below the original lowest panes and clear of existing mapped entrance fittings. There are **zero new doors** and no extra public furniture added merely to increase the count.

The prior complete source shells, source paint/materials, roof shapes, v188 base/eave strokes, doors, navigation and line buffers are retained. New frame colours, member dimensions, finish and lower shop-opening dimensions are explicit **display estimates**; the inherited v188 panes are themselves earlier display estimates, not surveyed architectural openings. No specific historic plaster, stone type, current tenant or facade paint is claimed.
