# Verified Berlin zebra crossings

V329 checks all Berlin records from the retained OpenStreetMap extract of 29 September 2026 and the official road-survey marking polygons with `FBF=3`. The latter are measured crossing bands from 2014–15. The polygon layer excludes Mitte, where the supplement uses explicitly zebra-tagged OpenStreetMap crossings. The separate Berlin register of installed crossings is used for conflict screening, since it also records crossings that have since been removed or replaced. [Straßenbefahrung metadata](https://gdi.berlin.de/geonetwork/geonetwork/api/records/7a97a60b-2c8f-3bb6-905c-66d26ee0a52a) · [Survey attributes](https://gdi.berlin.de/data/strassenbefahrung/docs/Datenformatbeschreibung_Stra%C3%9Fenbefahrung_2014.pdf) · [Crossing register](https://gdi.berlin.de/geonetwork/geonetwork/api/records/d9083c07-f571-3abb-9e36-5eac845086ca) · [OSM marking scheme](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Key:crossing:markings#Zebra_style_crossings)

The compiler associates point-only crossings with a connected, unambiguous road axis. It deduplicates node and way tags and respects the existing V206, V210, V235 and V249 markings. Both presentations use the retained road triangles from their own packet. Native uses independently generated cardinal cells with full road support. Stripe spacing, widths lacking a source measurement, and the paint colour are display estimates. The Berlin survey alone establishes what was observed at the survey date.

The two lazy recipe files store 108 Drawn and 127 Minecraft packet plans. The shipping viewer’s additional U-Bahn bounds check skips both stored plans for `ring182-9_11`. In the normal viewer, 107 Drawn and 126 Minecraft packet plans remain eligible, with 153 and 178 distinct source records respectively. Together they represent 181 distinct source records, including 150 available in both modes. An island may split one physical crossing into multiple source records. The existing terrain, road meshes, navigation, and building geometry are retained. The evidence records unsupported surfaces and packets reserved for existing U-Bahn aperture or Rosenthaler ground owners. The Berlin survey establishes historical coverage. The additions show supported model locations.

The Berlin datasets use [Datenlizenz Deutschland Zero 2.0](https://www.govdata.de/dl-de/zero-2-0). OpenStreetMap data are © OpenStreetMap contributors under [ODbL 1.0](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). The source snapshot, including original service request URLs and response fingerprints, is in `geo_data/regierungsviertel/zebra-crossings-v329-source.json.gz`. The companion evidence file records all admitted footprints, exact packet hashes and the named exclusions.

Rebuild the recipes with `uv run --no-sync python -m scripts.build_zebra_crossings_v329` and then `bun scripts/finalize-zebra-crossings-v329.ts` from `src/app`. The offline compiler uses the archived V326 floor preflight and the published V328 packet manifest. The finalizer verifies the exact current original packets and binds their raw navigation guards. Use `--check` with the finalizer to check without rewriting the recipes.
