# Regierungsviertel materials and loading · v311

The Reichstag’s retained masonry and its authored mouldings now use adjoining
grey-beige sandstone tones. The roof terrace and the darker tower caps have
separate colours. The Bundestag’s account of the original sandstone fabric and
the owner’s west-front photograph (v285) informed the choice.
[Bundestag building history](https://www.bundestag.de/parlament/geschichte/schauplaetze/reichstag_teilung)

The Chancellery’s frame and office bands use warm light-grey concrete and
muted blue-grey glazing. The official exterior views and the owner’s v287
Ehrenhof photographs inform the daylight palette.
[Federal Government architecture and photography](https://www.bundesregierung.de/breg-de/bundesregierung/bundeskanzleramt/geschichte-bundeskanzleramt-975040)

The Brandenburg Gate’s Doric columns, reliefs and recesses use separate
sandstone values. The Quadriga and pavilion roofs use a muted green copper
patina. The restoration report identifies the stone and the monument register
documents the gate and sculpture. The colour choices are illustrative daylight
values. The referenced photographs remain external.
[Restoration report](https://steinhof-restaurierung.de/projekt/brandenburger-tor-farbanschlag/)
[Berlin monument register](https://denkmaldatenbank.berlin.de/daobj.php?obj_dok_nr=09065019)
