# Huxleys entrance follow-up

This is an evidence audit only. No runtime mesh, packet, source retirement,
navigation change or new entrance is introduced by this follow-up.

The current [venue operator](https://huxleysneuewelt.de/information) confirms
Hasenheide 107–113. The retained complete v238 exterior is parent
`DEBE08YYF00009PW`, OSM way `30255865`, in packet `ring182-6_7`.
Its 15 measured parts and the later v294 stucco fittings remain intact.

The [entrance photograph published by Berliner Morgenpost](https://www.morgenpost.de/berlin/article404286900/huxleys-neue-welt-parken-anfahrt-garderobe-programm.html),
dated 9 April 2025 and credited there to Charlotte Bauer / Unbekannt, was
inspected directly. It shows red-brown glazed double doors and an upper light,
a dark nameboard and a thin light glazed canopy on metal members. The close
crop establishes appearance but supplies neither coordinates nor enough of
the complete elevation to match one particular source-wall bay unambiguously.
No image pixels or poster artwork are copied into the viewer.

A fresh [bounded OSM API extract](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map?bbox=13.4200,52.4855,13.4230,52.4875)
on 15 November 2026 still places venue POI `975700421` at scene XZ
`[3333.641,3679.885]`, inside the building complex rather than on its street
wall. It is not an entrance record. Entrance node `9104186570`, at
`[3262.846,3681.108]`, is west of this source body and cannot locate the pictured
Huxleys portal. The retained v238 `entrances` array remains empty; it is not
silently populated from the venue POI or an unrelated nearby doorway.

The property operator's [site directory](https://neuewelt-berlin.de/store-directory/)
provides a February 2020 schematic ground-floor plan of building I. It places
Huxleys among several other uses and marks multiple entrances. The plan has no
coordinate grid or survey scale, and does not identify the photographed door
against the measured v238 wall subdivisions. Its historical tenants are not
used as evidence of current occupancy. The venue operator's
[September 2019 technical specification](https://channelmusic.de/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/Venue-Specs-Huxleys-09-2019.pdf)
also supplies internal/parking plans, but no surveyed location of this public
street portal.

The remaining gap is the exact exterior doorway location and its corresponding
Drawn and Native wall hosts. A later addition should pin that bay against the
actual v238 packet, retain every existing source part and share its existing
streaming lifetime. This audit does not claim that the full requested Huxleys
refinement has been completed.
