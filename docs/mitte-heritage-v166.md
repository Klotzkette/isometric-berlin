# Mitte parks and historical buildings — v1.0.66

Step 10, within the existing bounds. The six mapped areas are Weinbergspark,
Monbijoupark, Krausnickpark, Elisabeth-Friedhof, Sophien II and Sophienkirchhof.
The package retains 20 complete LoD2 parents, 29 parts and all 461 source wall
and roof surfaces, alongside 193 mapped paths, 65 barriers and 430 point objects.

## Source hierarchy and limits

Berlin LoD2 supplies metric building outlines and roofs. The cached
`berlin-260929.osm.pbf` supplies park boundaries, paths, walls, trees, furniture
and grave anchors. Source and navigation JSON are generated by
`build_mitte_heritage_v166.py`; the separate evidence file retains selected
identities, original ground heights, applied scene offsets and previous owners.
The existing scene datum (5.2 m inside the detailed core, 3 m outside) is retained;
this is not a new terrain survey of the Weinberg slope.

Kaufhaus Jandorf is OSM 33791235 and Landesdenkmalamt 09090038, with its rounded
corner, tall selling-floor windows, upper arches and copper roof rider. St.
Elisabeth is OSM 27214404 and Landesdenkmalamt 09010143, with six square Doric
piers and five open intervals. It is the Schinkel church by Pappelplatz; no church
is invented in Pappelallee. The complete measured envelopes stay intact.
See [the source-separated ornament contract](mitte-ornaments-v166.md) for Heine,
Jandorf's photograph-estimated crown and the Elisabeth portico.

Only individually mapped graves are placed: Walter Kollo, Bechstein and Max
Stirner. Their small generic stone forms are display estimates, not surveyed
portraits of the graves. No dense field of invented grave positions is generated.
Chapel, park-house and boundary geometry remains source-bound. Existing detailed
Rosenthaler Platz buildings are excluded from this ownership set.

## Display and preservation

The drawn modes use identical complete source triangles, 6,627 batched facade
subdivisions and mapped landscape on pointer and touch. Rounded, faceted crowns
are used for the added park trees. Source-aligned window spacing, furniture and
unpublished barrier heights are explicitly approximate recognition detail.

Minecraft has a separate orthogonal surface reading: 9,559 source runs and 8,025
landscape runs, plus windows, ornaments and small objects. Its roof lookup uses
flooring for negative Z coordinates. The earlier user-requested Minecraft tree
reduction keeps one third of the new outer tree anchors; all four drawn modes
retain all anchors. Existing inner official trees remain under their old owner.

Nine exact old core prism identities and eleven outer coarse owners are retired
only where the complete new source parts take their place. Existing roads, water,
bridges and unrelated packet triangles/navigation are preserved by the separate
city-refinement audit. Source payloads are texture-free; inspected photographs
remain reference-only with per-file credits in both attribution manifests.
