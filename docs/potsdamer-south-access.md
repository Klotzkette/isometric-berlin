# Potsdamer Platz public access at the south pavilion

Both drawn entrance pavilions now render their roofs and side panes as
transparent glass. The complete source footprints, roof elevations, exterior
steel grids and signs still define their shape. The south pavilion uses its
retained LoD2 reference `DEBE01YYK0000BRX`, entrance node `1576240058` and the
owner's street photographs documented in
[Potsdamer Platz from owner photographs](potsdamer-photos-v284.md).

S-Bahn Berlin lists four escalators at Pavillon Süd. The model has four moving
flights in opposing pairs, two fixed stair lanes, an open guarded stairwell and
a lower landing. The 17 m runs, tread widths, fitting positions and movement
directions are display estimates. The lower model level uses the retained
LoD2 minimum at −0.518 m in viewer coordinates. The glass pavilion at the
surface remains the source model with a 14.178 m absolute roof elevation.
[Operator's equipment register](https://sbahn.berlin/fahren/bahnhofsuebersicht/potsdamer-platz/?jumpTo=equipment)

The stairwell cuts only the affected ground slabs and paving triangles.
Walking and flying use the admitted stairwell, landing and guards. Minecraft
uses cardinal blocks and an independently stepped terrain opening. Motion
shares the existing station clock and follows its visibility, suspension and
reduced-motion rules. Geometry contains no reference-image pixels.

The operator separately lists four escalators at Pavillon Nord and connections
from the Mittelpassarelle to Leipziger Straße and the S-Bahn platforms.
The architect describes a separate rectangular void in the Passarelle and
four regional station levels. Those sources establish the circulation topology.
The [east gallery of the Mittelpassarelle](potsdamer-mittelpassarelle.md) connects
the two Leipziger Platz street stairs. The north native pavilion and the
remaining links to the passage and platforms are outside that local route.

[Regional station architect](https://www.hoe-architects.com/projekte/regionalbahnhof-potsdamer-platz-berlin/)

The 20 focused tests cover the actual source prisms and terrain, both
stair-lane walks down and back, lower-landing flight clearance, the exact
adjacent pavement, transparent glazing, both native column receipts and the
existing Hauptbahnhof motion and entrance fittings.

## Connected public circulation

The retained glass shells now frame the mapped public stairs. The north
pavilion reaches level −1 by fixed ways 237683333 and 241572602, with
landing 241572603. Its gallery follows 464904363, 464904367 and 464904407
to the western opening of the Mittelpassarelle at viewer coordinates
approximately `[310.82, −0.518, 1029.93]`.

The south pavilion reaches level −1 by the fixed ways 237526535 and
241570276 with landing 241570278. It continues through 464904390 to the
split fixed stairs 237526530 and 241570409 and their landing 241570408.
The public lower gallery follows 464904244/245, 464904259, 464904241 and
464904228. Fixed stair 464904229 returns to level −1 and passage 464904404
reaches the same western opening. Separate fixed stairs and two escalators
in the north pavilion also connect −1 and −2. The lining has bounded
openings at all twelve mapped approaches toward the regional stairways
(OSM 464904235–240 and 464904251–256).

The centerlines, stair counts where mapped and direction tags come from
the Geofabrik Berlin OpenStreetMap extract of 29 September 2026. Both glass
pavilions serve the Regionalbahnhof in the architects' project record.
The operator separately confirms four escalators under each pavilion.
The public lower walkway is modeled at −3.918 m. The intermediate landings,
widths and headroom are display estimates. Drawn and Native use separate
floor partitions, and share the source points and level topology. The North
Native model transfers only its measured prism and the corresponding 47
exact source cells. The drawn pavilion follows the existing measured envelope.
The protected glass walls, retained paving, doorway approaches and steps
are checked with actual source payloads. Tests walk both routes in both
directions and sweep the standard 62 cm flight sphere through both routes.
