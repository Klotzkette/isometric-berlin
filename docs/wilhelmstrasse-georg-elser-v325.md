# Wilhelmstraße and Georg Elser memorial context

This correction follows the complete current Wilhelmstraße chain from
Reichstagufer to Hallesches Ufer, approximately 2.407 km. It improves selected
source-bound street fronts throughout that corridor and recomposes the existing
nineteen housing families around the Holocaust Memorial and An der Kolonnade.
It does not claim a new survey of every building on the street.

The housing composition uses shared family storeys, continuous ground floors,
and explicit ordinary-window and oriel columns. Small source wall sheets inherit
the same storeys rather than restarting a grid. The additional nine compositions
cover eleven complete families: Wilhelmstraße 65/67, 43, Block 9 at 110–113,
Rossi 36–38, the former school/current Kita 116/117, De Rossi 119, terraces
120/121 and Fehling/Gogel 149/150. Original body mass, roofs, courtyards, passages,
source colors and navigation remain intact. In particular, joinery does not
resolve a coarse original extrusion at Fehling/Gogel; its mass should not be
represented as photographically reconstructed.

Drawn fittings follow the original wall masks. Native fittings use independently
verified complete cardinal wall supports from the actual original Native
constructor. A covered optional short return is omitted rather than exposing a
new plane. Touch retains the full new fitting coverage. Generic fittings yield
only after the actual source-bound replacement is complete, with exact rollback
on cancellation or source revocation. Northern Native corrections share one
original-host transaction with the earlier housing correction; southern Core
corrections share the existing office/university transaction.

## Built Georg Elser memorial

The existing sculpture keeps its mapped anchor, 17 m height and three-layer
profile. The built photographic evidence supports two flush illuminated quote
bands, with subdued gray-green glass, warm lettering and granite edging. A
third strip appears in an artist's proposal and is not modeled as built.

The transparent information stele has its own mapped position,
[OSM node 11395350229](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/11395350229), about nine
metres west of the sculpture. Its narrow dark frame, red title, small bilingual
history column and five abstract monochrome picture fields follow the overall
built composition. No historical photograph is reproduced in the product. Band
lengths, offsets, stele dimensions and heading are photograph-proportioned display
estimates; the board's geographic point is source-bound.

## Storage and scope

The complete source values remain unchanged by lossless encoding. Previously
eager source objects retain ordinary strong own-data properties and named/default
alias identity. New immutable constructor recipes use the established mode-local
codec. No public photographic textures, new runtime image requests, recurring
scene timers, larger streaming limits or reduced touch detail are introduced.
All six views and 93 tour stops remain part of the release checks.
