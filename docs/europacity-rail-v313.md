# Europacity railway junction and Perleberger Straße

The viewer follows three distinct double-track alignments between Europacity and the Ringbahn. The western S-Bahn curve (6017) passes below Perleberger Straße, the eastern S-Bahn curve (6019) crosses it on the steel arch, and the separately supported Fernbahn flyover (6171) rises above both. The eastern curve is part of the S15 service via Wedding to Gesundbrunnen. The western connection is represented as track geometry. [S-Bahn Berlin](https://sbahn.berlin/aktuelles/artikel/neue-s-bahn-linie-s15-geht-in-betrieb/), [Deutsche Bahn](https://www.deutschebahn.com/de/presse/presse-regional/pr-berlin-de/aktuell/presseinformationen/Neue-S-Bahn-Linie-S15-faehrt-ab-jetzt-zwischen-Gesundbrunnen-und-Hauptbahnhof--13900884), [Vössing project description](https://www.voessing.de/de/projekte/s21-berlin---westliche-und-ostliche-nordringanbindung-bis-hauptbahnhof-tief~pr455?object=455)

The city bridge register records Perleberger Brücke as steel construction, 105.19 m long and 20.4 m wide. The viewer uses the mapped road axis and its approach connections. The OSM centreline segment between the mapped endpoints is about 107 m. The model includes four traffic lanes, pedestrian and cycle bands, guardrails, abutments and both grade transitions. The Drawn road surface and the cardinal Native road cells each provide their own navigation floor. The height hint preserves movement under the bridge at the lower level. [Berlin bridge register, structure 3446154](https://www.berlin.de/sen/uvk/_assets/verkehr/infrastruktur/brueckenbau/masterplan-bruecken-berlin/mpb_anhang_1_brueckenliste_bestand.pdf?ts=1779280609), [OpenStreetMap bridge axis](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/4677616)

The retained 29 September 2026 Berlin OSM extract provides each track axis, the road and the mapped exclusion features used to place supports. The 2025 Berlin bDOM supplies the observed elevations at 1 m resolution in EPSG:25833 and DHHN2016. Scene coordinates subtract 30 m from NHN. The evidence archive `geo_data/regierungsviertel/europacity-rail-v313-source.json.gz` pins the source downloads, retains 27 selected way records and the measured lateral samples for 24 ways, and records the exact previous rail parts replaced. At obscured crossings, the nearby observations and relative order inform the rendered elevations. Deck sections, support positions, details and the joins to the existing viewer terrain are display estimates.
