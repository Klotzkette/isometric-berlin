# Wilhelm street fronts: nine finite photographic treatments (V325)

The additional model covers eleven audited families in nine visual groups from northern Wilhelmstraße through the central GDV front to the southern IBA buildings. It is separate from the unchanged scope of the V324 housing families. Source bodies, roofs, courts, mapped doors and navigation remain under their original owners. The photographic material is reference evidence; no image pixels, crops, signs or photographic textures are bundled.

The eleven complete families retain 89 source leaves and 87 actual original prism records. The drawn recipe uses 22 directed runs and 163 complete actual wall patches. The independent native recipe uses complete original cardinal wall unions, positive original voxel intervals and all four exposure neighbours. Native run 11 is physically unexposed in the retained 4 m representation; its main run 10 remains covered. Every named family has positive drawn and native coverage.

## Composition and limits

The compiler creates common family storeys and one oriented set of bay axes per complete run. The nine named handlers distinguish warm brick and terracotta courses, folded glass cues, a tall colonnade rhythm, green Rossi glazing, Grupo 2C slit windows, segmental school arches, De Rossi upper colour fields, terrace/pergola cues and pale curved parapet bands. These small dimensions, pane divisions, tones and display cues are interpretations guided by the references. Original source planes and holes constrain every complete serialized fitting rectangle. They are not a surveyed reconstruction of every window.

Portal and entrance ornament needs a positively located entrance anchor. No source-backed portal centre has been established in these inputs, so the compiler adds no invented doorway, passage or route. A rectangular box renderer approximates Wilhelmstraße 65 glass folding through shallow stepped leaves; it does not claim measured glass-plane angles. Fehling/Gogel retains its coarse original OSM body, including the original maximum-height extrusion. The new bands do not make that pre-existing mass a fully photo-accurate reconstruction of the lower wings.

The current finite recipes contain 20,324 drawn and 18,649 native boxes, in two batches per mode with identical full/touch detail. All 27 foreign contributor records are bound in both modes before admitting the new fields. Native admission additionally checks all required original packets and full RLE interval/list topology; full world containers remain weakly held. Private model/source/pose/word/material receipts, original shared previous-owner and Government/Core transactions control publication and revoke the new model on failure. There is no second mutation owner on the eight shared Alt-Mitte meshes.

`build_wilhelm_street_fronts_v325.py` consumes the reviewed complete actual-wall input, full family records, independent native wall unions and the nine-photo plan. Its input and native-support SHA checks, complete polygon/hole checks, neighbour tests and post-serialization proof run before producing external review candidates. `wilhelm_street_photo_styles_v325.py` contains the finite treatments. No older source fixture or runtime cache lifetime is changed.
