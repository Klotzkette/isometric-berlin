# Furnished Reichstag section and basement

Step 10, v1.0.199. The exterior click opens a longitudinal section with furnished
side wings as well as the plenary chamber. **Räume / Rooms** brings the furnished
wing closer without changing the section. **Untergeschoss / Basement** removes
the upper building and its bounded terrain cover for a closer lower-level view.
**Verbindungen / Connections** additionally reveals the represented underground
links. Closing restores the complete scene; walking retains its earlier public
entrance, chamber and dome routes and closes this architectural exhibit.

The foot passages are distinct from the vehicle/goods system. Nothing in these
display views asserts that restricted parliamentary circulation is open to the
public. See the [furnished-wing evidence](reichstag-rooms-v319.md) and
[basement/connection evidence](reichstag-underground-v319.md) for individual
primary references, measured source anchors and the limits of the reconstruction.

drawn/native exhibit families are cached on desktop; only the matching family
is visible. The selected view persists through a mobile city-family remount.
Existing world GPU eviction/upload handling owns these batches normally, and
closing/disposal restores the original terrain, exterior and material state.
