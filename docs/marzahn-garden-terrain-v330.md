# Measured terrain for Gärten der Welt and Kienbergpark

The V330 terrain uses the official Erholungspark Marzahn polygon and ten named GRIS areas covering Kienberg, its terraces and plateau, Wuhletal, and the eastern park connection. The official polygons cover approximately 42.7 ha and 61.9 ha respectively. The three mapped ropeway stations, Wuhleteich, and short sections of verified eastern paths complete the ground domain. The OpenStreetMap boundary of Gärten der Welt is retained separately for comparison. The developer’s route information distinguishes the paid garden from the public Kienbergpark and its east station. [Official garden overview](https://gruen-berlin.de/projekte/parks/gaerten-der-welt/) · [Official Kienbergpark overview](https://gruen-berlin.de/projekte/parks/kienbergpark/) · [Route and stations](https://gruen-berlin.de/projekte/infrastruktur/seilbahn/linie-stationen)

The compiler checks the actual previous Drawn and Minecraft packet geometry records against the immutable v1.0.210 manifest. Ground, water, roads, bridges and building footprints with positive overlap are subtracted before building the new domain. For the final scope, the earlier packet inventory contributes zero square metres of overlap. The new Drawn source domain is approximately 1,070,254 m². The Native domain uses complete, cardinal metre cells inside that domain and covers 1,058,550 m². The compiler merges row strips even where neighboring edges meet only in part, preserving complete interior cells. The land query excludes the mapped water in both modes.

The source uses official one-metre ATKIS DGM1 in EPSG:25833 and heights in DHHN2016. It retains the four archive hashes and a fingerprint of all sampled one-metre values. The scene transformation is `x=E−389500, z=5820000−N, y=NHN−30m+0.10m`, consistent with the existing U-Bahn approach elevations. The four-metre Drawn grid uses two planes per cell with a northwest-to-southeast diagonal. The Native platform heights use an independent DGM sample at each four-metre cell centre, rounded to decimetres. Drawn control heights include 22.80 m at the west cable station, 25.65 m at the east station, and 71.99 m near the Kienberg summit. These are scene heights rather than metres above sea level. [Official DGM metadata](https://gdi.berlin.de/geonetwork/geonetwork/api/records/fa02f9e1-a0df-3be1-b0aa-bc624c0c7ff5)

The source records 519 at-grade path courses and 17 elevated paths separately. Ground corridors follow the mapped edges of the named sites. Where no width is mapped, the corridor width is a display estimate. The eastern corridor follows evidenced at-grade paths and crossings. Any missing final connection to an entrance remains unresolved. The November 2026 operator notice separately documents temporary closures at the north circular trail, Wolkenweg and Tälchenbrücke. [Current operator closure notice](https://www.gaertenderwelt.de/service/news/detail/eingeschraenkte-wegeverbindung-im-kienbergpark-1/)

The compact terrain source is `geo_data/regierungsviertel/marzahn-garden-terrain-v330-source.json.gz`. Rebuild it with `uv run --no-sync python -m scripts.marzahn_garden_terrain_v330` using the pinned ignored Geoportal caches, GRIS snapshot and retained 29 September 2026 OpenStreetMap extract. The official terrain and GRIS data use [Datenlizenz Deutschland Zero 2.0](https://www.govdata.de/dl-de/zero-2-0).
