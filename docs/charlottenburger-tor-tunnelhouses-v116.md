# Charlottenburger Tor and Großer Stern tunnel houses

Pipeline step 10. This pass replaces the generic Charlottenburger Tor blocks
and adds the four missing pedestrian-tunnel gatehouses around the Siegessäule.

## Charlottenburger Tor

The [district's building history](https://www.berlin.de/ba-charlottenburg-wilmersdorf/ueber-den-bezirk/bauwerke/artikel.1368662.php)
identifies Bernhard Schaede's 1907–08 gate, its colonnaded composition,
Friedrich I and Sophie Charlotte bronzes, and the 1937 widening from 20 m to
34 m. The previous model incorrectly placed two tall blocks east and west
along the road. The two new wings stand north and south of the road with end
pylons, four-column openings, layered entablatures, attic relief cues, the two
principal bronze figures and restrained roof groups.

The 34 m opening and landmark anchor are source facts. Column diameter,
cornice subdivision and simplified sculpture anatomy are bounded procedural
presentation geometry rather than a facade or sculpture survey.

## Großer Stern tunnel entrances

OSM ways `106952577`, `106952579`, `106953928` and `106953934` retain the four
separate civic footprints, two levels and hipped-roof identity. Their projected
centres are used without relocation. Each receives a visible dark tunnel
mouth, four-column portico, triangular pediment, hipped roof and shallow stair
flight. The local ground height follows the committed OSM park-path surface.

The public architectural record identifies the gatehouses as the 1941
neoclassical entrances to the pedestrian tunnels at the Siegessäule. Local
door, column and pediment dimensions remain reference-bounded presentation
fits. No claim is made that these are measured elevations.

Geometry QA uses actual Three.js triangles and orthographic software views;
it is not a browser screenshot. The review caught and corrected both a ground
height mismatch and an over-scaled roof transform before release testing.
