# PETRI Berlin exterior

The old context body at Gertraudenstraße 8 is a 9 m display fallback, not a
measured LoD2 height. The current building opened in June 2025. This update
preserves its exact source footprint and lower body and adds the completed
exterior: pale plaster, a tall ground-level colonnade treatment, three rows
of upper windows and an open roof loggia. The roughly 24 m height comes from
the official architecture documentation and remains an approximation, not a
new survey measurement. Bay dimensions, relief depths and colours are visual
model estimates checked against the completed building's handover photograph.

- [Berlin's architecture documentation, pp. 37–43](https://www.berlin.de/sen/stadtentwicklung/_assets/staedtebau/berliner-mitte/petriplatz/ahp-dokumentation-wettbewerb.pdf)
- [Completed building, handover 2024](https://www.berlin.de/landesdenkmalamt/aktivitaeten/kurzmeldungen/2024/uebergabe-petri-1460979.php)
- [BIM: PETRI opened, 2025](https://www.bim-berlin.de/landesimmobilien/news/newsdetail/petri-berlin-eroeffnet)
- [Architect's completed project](https://www.nagler-architekten.de/projekt-daten/archiv-ansicht/archaeologisches-besucherzentrum-berlin/)
- [OSM footprint, way 972871827](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/972871827)

The 36 Native columns are independently pinned to the current 4 m payload;
their actual cardinal exposed faces receive the new detail. No old column,
source polygon, navigation obstacle or city packet is removed. All source
fields and every selected Native tuple must match before admission. The lower
source body remains, so the ground arcade is exterior relief and does not
grant access to an invented museum interior. No source photograph, plan,
imagery tile or font file is redistributed.

The Native upper opening is classified by the nearest original footprint
edge and its outward normal. This keeps the loggia on the same front as Drawn
and leaves both neighbouring return walls closed. Output raycasts verify the
open front and closed returns; all Native instance axes remain cardinal.
