# Berlin tax and citizen offices in V355

The sources record 23 Finanzamt addresses and 45 Bürgeramt or Bürgerbüro addresses in all 12 boroughs, checked on 16 November 2026. These are physical sites, not counts of offices served or verified street doors.

The [Service Berlin tax directory](https://service.berlin.de/standorte/finanzaemter/) and [citizen office directory](https://service.berlin.de/standorte/buergeraemter/) enumerate 29 and 114 service entries. The directory location IDs match the official map feed IDs exactly. For equal street, house number and postcode, alternate appointment types, document collection and shared authorities are recorded as aliases of the same address. The 29 tax rows account for all [24 tax authorities](https://www.berlin.de/sen/finanzen/steuern/) at 22 portal addresses. The additional [Mehringdamm 110/112 branch](https://www.berlin.de/sen/finanzen/steuern/finanzaemter/friedrichshain-kreuzberg/) has no public counter and is located by the two exact official RBS points. The [Körperschaften II branch](https://www.berlin.de/sen/finanzen/steuern/finanzaemter/finanzamt-fuer-koerperschaften-ii/) shares the Lichtenberg address and does not add a second feature there.

Of the 114 citizen office entries, 62 describe other services at the fixed sites. Seven special entries are retained separately as evidence: two mobile-service administrative locations, two scheduled mobile services at senior centers, one back office, one education and participation office, and one housing-benefit advice office. One mobile-service entry has reversed latitude and longitude in the source. The normalized point and original coordinate order are both preserved. The underlying portal records distinguish each address or service.

The [retained OpenStreetMap Berlin extract](https://download.geofabrik.de/europe/germany/berlin-261113.osm.pbf) supplies a unique mapped building polygon for each of the 68 addresses: 27 complete address matches, 39 polygons containing the source point, and 2 matches using independent official points. When two polygons share an exact address, the original point must fall within exactly one of them. The primary Klosterstraße and Pankow portal points are outside a uniquely identified OSM building. The [Mitte](https://www.berlin.de/ba-mitte/politik-und-verwaltung/aemter/amt-fuer-buergerdienste/buergeraemter/artikel.244730.php) and [Pankow](https://www.berlin.de/ba-pankow/politik-und-verwaltung/aemter/amt-fuer-buergerdienste/buergeramt/) maps and [official RBS addresses](https://gdi.berlin.de/geonetwork/geonetwork/api/records/c43966c4-95b0-39f8-8b8c-545acfa7fbf9) retain the alternative points and corroborate the postal addresses. No nearest-building extrapolation is used. 20 mapped buildings have at least one explicitly tagged main entrance; the 17 with exactly one also receive a single `entrance` record. These identify the building's entrance. A shared building may have a different entrance to the service suite.

`aemter-v355-source.json.gz` is source evidence for production-owner checks. Its GeoJSON polygons are WGS84, its example facade positions use the existing EPSG:25833 world origin, and its official map points are approximate location coordinates. A mapped shell still has to agree with the retained production shell before adding a fitting. Seventeen sites refer to the existing V227 Rathaus construction and its public premises. Those links establish quarter-level identity; they are not permissions to remove a building, reveal or extrapolate interiors, or paint over prior authored facade details.

The tall upper windows differ from pairs on the three lower rows. The Charlottenburg recipe places the three inner dark window courses on individual, proven retained wall spans of host `-10_1`. Its independent cardinal Native face uses a separate measured 20 m span. The patterned windows, recess depth and remaining facade are described by the reference, while the additive recipe retains the existing measured shell, stair bays and masonry pier strips. Both modes passed the production wall and unchanged predecessor checks.

## Reproduction

Use `uv run python -m scripts.build_aemter_v355 SCRATCH --fetch --out SCRATCH/base.json.gz` to retrieve new portal snapshots. For the 16 November receipt the date is `--date 2026-11-16` and the four existing snapshots are used without `--fetch`. `scripts.add_aemter_evidence_v355` reads the narrow borough pages and photo references named in its source dictionary, the two SenFin pages and `rbs-selected.json`. The latter can be refreshed with `--fetch-rbs`. Run `uv run python -m scripts.add_aemter_evidence_v355 SCRATCH/base.json.gz SCRATCH SCRATCH/supplemented.json.gz`, then `uv run python -m scripts.match_aemter_v355 SCRATCH/supplemented.json.gz geo_data/regierungsviertel/aemter-v355-source.json.gz` to add exact OSM shapes and entrances using the retained PBF. `uv run pytest tests/test_aemter_v355.py` checks address accounting, source joins and photo/entrance consistency.
