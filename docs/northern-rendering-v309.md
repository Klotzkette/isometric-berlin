# Northern sites, terrain and desktop loading

The northern site bundle joins the school and parks, rail stations and
Europasportpark, clinical buildings, and the two Gesundbrunnen road bridges.
Its geometry loads when an existing visible city tile or a named site needs it.
The ordinary source city remains available until the complete replacement,
terrain, retained objects and navigation are ready together.

## Terrain and source ownership

The complete initial raster, the real separate worker ground/road batches and
the native raster are audited in their actual client preparation order. A
staged transaction keeps their original geometry and instance references until
all required models and retained objects pass admission. Every original
triangle outside the bounded replacement has one retained owner. Badstraße's
additional Core correction is a separate, disjoint delta; the original Core
recipes and outside fragments remain unchanged.

Navigation uses the actual rendered contact surfaces. Sloped bridge decks have
local upper and lower planes, leaving the rail cutting open underneath. A road
deck is never treated as the ground below it. Native terrain uses centimetre
quantization of independently constructed cardinal faces; drawn terrain is not
relabelled as a native model. Architectural heights and source roof shapes keep
their own source precision.

The runtime source adapter in `coreTerrainActualV310.json` binds the complete
production constructors, including their actual tunnel and surface options and
the final native fidelity-ground restoration. Its complete main raster has
125,364 instances; the final native raster has 184,956. The 3,078 main and 784
native instances in the local terrain transfer retain every original matrix
and paint word. Only their global instance guards and retirement ordinals
change. The original capture variants remain historical controls; all twenty
inside/outside geometry and navigation streams are reused byte for byte.

The original park Worker has two independently reproduced triangulations in
JavaScriptCore and V8. A separately pinned V8 recipe recognises the complete
actual position/index buffers. Its different 600-to-598-triangle block lies
outside the terrain transfer; all 184,049 transferred triangles retain their
exact Float32 position words and winding. Only 918 subsequent retirement
ordinals shift by two. The actual browser's original buffers remain untouched
outside the transfer, and existing replacement/outside/navigation streams are
reused. Admission uses exact source guards, with no browser-name selection or
numeric tolerance; unknown inputs retain the original city.

Each world family retains its own original ground sampler before publication,
including a warm switch that reuses the existing walking environment. Streamed
ink terrain adjustments receive only their audited source prefix; subsequently
appended northern-quarter lines retain their independently prepared coordinates.

Retirement of old hospital facades is scoped to the exact retained world.
The shared outline ward still has one canonical owner across the scene. After
a genuine outline rebuild it can be admitted afresh only with complete source
proof, unchanged facades and the same retained outline host; attached manual
disposal, altered geometry and duplicate owners still revoke admission.

The measured Swinemünder bridge approach and the estimated transition to the
older scene's ground datum are documented separately in the rail source notes.
That display fit does not claim that the real street has the fitted gradient.
It retains the existing whole building owners and all source material values.

## Loading and graphics resources

The opening screen reports actual loading phases, bytes or completed work.
Work without a known total has an activity indicator. There is no simulated
combined percentage or fixed jump from 0 to 50 to 83 percent. Construction
checkpoints let the browser paint progress and process input between batches;
this does not shorten a measurement merely by declaring the city ready earlier.

The finite northern sources and the large preceding neighbourhood recipes are
loaded lazily. Their compressed source transport reconstructs the same values.
The additional Float32 word encodings preserve every stored bit, including
signed zero. They change neither source colours nor triangle order. The two
explicitly documented new native contact coordinates needed a one-word plane
correction; that correction has a separate reversible receipt from transport.

GPU retirement and authored resource disposal have different lifetimes. Only
an eligible, registered retirement inside the two residency managers can release
GPU handles while retaining source admission. Geometry or instance-buffer
replacement retires the observed old handles before rebinding, then registers
the new references and warms them normally. A manual disposal, changed pose,
foreign material, changed buffer or incomplete bundle closes admission and
restores the old city. The private exception has no writable public flag.

The same lifetime checks recognise the exact contour-line rank assigned by
the viewer's real stable-ink pass. A private identity binding records that
assignment; a public tag or an arbitrary render-order edit cannot admit a
changed line.

These changes retain full touch geometry, native face construction, all six
presentations, existing draw distances, shadows and quality settings. The
existing packet, memory, worker, shell and offline-package limits still apply.

## Verification scope

The tests use real settled initial/worker/native source batches, actual client
cuts, parked/interleaved buffers, the real Three.js WebGL attribute/object
lifecycle, mode changes, cancellation and rollback. Whole source words and
outside complements are checked separately from metadata lifetime checks.
Repeated live checks do not repeatedly hash megabytes of unchanged attributes.

Desktop measurements use an external timing probe against complete static
packages at the same camera view. The probe records visible loading text,
resource sizes, long tasks and animation-frame intervals. It is not part of
the delivered viewer and does not measure physical GPU memory or prove a frame
rate on every PC. Final release evidence records the actual measured timings;
no general speed factor is inferred from one local run.
