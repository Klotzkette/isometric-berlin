Their full original compressed bytes, complete real source-wall triangles,

actual constructors, after their normal client passes. Distant coverage has a
separate exact source-owner receipt.

its false3m LoD2 height. The lower-block and upper setback zones retain all three
original holes and additionally subtract both complete low court leaves,
including the fourth actual courtyard. The existing family offset −2.775 m is
reused: source top 8.775 m corresponds to the actual delivered 6 m roof. The separate
entrance tower and all other measured leaves stay unchanged. The higher link
rectangle and two-floor setback division are architectural interpretations,
not newly surveyed plan edges.

Württembergische Straße 6 keeps the complete old low complex. The narrow exact
OSM tower footprint alone rises from its actual delivered 29.25 m top, using the
ground-to-ground +0.213 m fit. The independent bDOM roof target
is 98.8 m NHN. Historical architectural height and absolute roof altitude are
not interchangeable measurements.

Native roofs are independent cardinal 2 m surface columns, with eighth-metre
stepped readings of measured sloping Oranien roofs. A full cell must clear every
source court; no low courtyard becomes a generic high tower. Roof and collision
surfaces use the same compiled footprints and represented heights.

Small window bays, fitting dimensions and material tones remain explicit public
reference estimates. This is a finite exterior model, not a photographic survey.
No reference pixels, new texture, timer or coverage are bundled. The 93-stop tour,
all six views, equal drawn touch detail and existing runtime/package limits remain.
