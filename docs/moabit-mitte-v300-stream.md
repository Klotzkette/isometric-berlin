# Jägerstraße retained-source fronts — Step 10 / v300

This finite companion refines **four distinct street fronts** on the complete
existing Alt-Mitte measured bodies: Jägerstraße 27, 28, 29–31 and 32. The
six-storey historic/modern composition at 27, narrow neo-Baroque house with its
oriel at 28, low six-axis house at 29–31, and broad seven-axis stone-pier front
at 32 receive separate compositions. New glazing, arch reveals, sash divisions,
sills, belt courses, rustication, balconets and fluted piers follow those
references. They are geometric interpretations, with unmeasured dimensions
explicitly estimated. Roof bodies and all original roof detail remain intact.

The source is the complete retained `alt-mitte-v169` parent/part inventory and
its existing appearance baseline. The same four parents are already protected
from the older global fallback prisms. This companion therefore does not add a
second body and does not reinstate those prisms. It changes no navigation.

Exactly **1,048 old estimated window/surround/mullion/sill triangles** from the
four actual street fronts in `drawn:3_1` yield only after the full new mesh is
attached through the guarded v300 transaction. Their indices are proved by
replaying the original fitting recipe and matching its local centimetre
quantisation to the actual raw packet. No wall, roof or line triangle yields.
All original mesh fields, navigation and lines have exact runtime fingerprints.
The `minecraft:3_1` companion retires no native body triangle: its finer fittings
are independently projected onto actual cardinal faces of the resident native
source model. Every selected native sheet has an exact source triangle receipt.
Drawn and native share photographic composition, not a rotated geometry copy.
Touch uses the full same additions. No descriptor or runtime limit changes.

References are reference-only. Jörg Zägel's three photographs on Wikimedia
Commons were taken on **26 July 2009** (CC BY-SA 3.0); the Landesdenkmalamt
Jägerstraße 28 image carries an embedded **1999** watermark. These are historical
architectural references and establish no 2026 paint or tenant survey. Author,
image URLs, dates, licence and precise uses are recorded in the accompanying
`moabit-mitte-v300-stream-evidence.json.gz`. No pixels, image assets, logos or
photographic textures are distributed. Geoportal Berlin LoD2 is dl-de/zero-2-0;
© OpenStreetMap contributors supplies semantic context under ODbL-1.0.

09030023 was incorrect**: that ensemble lists Charlottenstraße 77, not 75/76.
75/76 is the modern Checkpoint Plaza building, while 77 is the distinct former
Roter Adler (individual LDA **09030025**). All three remain unchanged here.

introduce a speculative duplicate grid. The four verified Jäger compositions
take precedence over increasing the address count.
