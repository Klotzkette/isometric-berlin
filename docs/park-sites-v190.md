# Humboldthain and Friedrichshain v1.0.90

The existing complete park surfaces, trees and official DGM hills remain.
Additional low-density crowns appear strictly inside mapped woodland, avoiding
existing mapped trees, paths, water and buildings. These are clearly documented
woodland illustrations, not individual surveyed trees; Minecraft receives half
the additional density.
This bounded pass adds 227 source-mapped benches, 357 mapped fence/wall/hedge/
stair segments and the stone edges of seven mapped basins. It does not add
imaginary paths or flatten the hills. Furniture profiles, untagged bench
orientations, widths and material colours are illustrative.

The two surviving Humboldthain viewing turrets follow mapped railing ways
439035491 and 439035493, with concrete sides, platforms and closely spaced
metal railings. The former v182 bunker envelope remains. A three-metre
relative platform rise is a photo-guided display estimate, not a building
survey; no fictitious interior museum is added. Berliner Unterwelten operates
guided visits through the ruin, distinct from its nearby underground museum.

Photo inspected: Andreas Praefcke, *Berlin Humboldthain Aussichtspunkte Bunker*,
April 2009, CC BY 3.0. This dated exterior is a shape/material reference;
no pixels are bundled and no current access conditions are inferred from it.
All source IDs and official/operator references are in the evidence JSON.

Drawn and native representations use exact-count static instancing in separately
cullable park groups. Both touch and pointer devices receive the complete
representation; no textures, animation loops or larger runtime budgets are added.
