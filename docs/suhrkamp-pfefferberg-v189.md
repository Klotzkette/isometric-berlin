# Suhrkamp and Pfefferberg recognition, v1.0.89

Step 10 adds shallow architectural recognition on 45 measured wall rectangles
of eight existing source owners. No earlier building, surface, roof, terrain,
road, navigation record or packet is regenerated or removed. Both drawn device
profiles receive the same detail. Minecraft receives separate axis-aligned
surface blocks. No photograph, texture, font or protected plan is bundled.

## Identities and source roles

[Suhrkamp's current contact page](https://www.suhrkamp.de/kontakt-s-63) establishes
Torstraße 44. This is OSM way `716014884`, not the black L40 on the opposite
corner, OSM way `59051762`. The Commons photograph has a Linienstraße 34 caption;
that caption is retained as a source qualification rather than used to rename
the publisher's address. [Bundschuh's project account](https://bundschuh.net/project/suhrkamp-ensemble/)
identifies the three-part ensemble and public square. The
[DGI Bauwerk project record](https://www.baunetz-architekten.de/dgi-bauwerk/2462237/projekt/7179111)
supports seven publisher storeys, the two-storey connection, four-storey
residential block, exposed concrete, anodised aluminium and large fixed glazing.
Design: Bundschuh Architekten; execution and project management: DGI Bauwerk.

The two Suhrkamp parents already have all eight measured parts in the retained
v168 source/detail layer. Their source geometry does not justify a new shell.
This revision adds silver horizontal bands, narrow north-facing office panes,
broader south-facing fixed glazing and concrete-core material/joint cues on
existing walls. Floor subdivisions are fitted display estimates, particularly
on the low connecting parts and residential body; they are not a per-window
survey. The complete irregular source roof and original public-space gaps stay.

The close review exposed retained generic window grids crossing the new large
panes. Each of the ten accepted Suhrkamp wall rectangles therefore has a thin
local facing, inset 2 cm from that rectangle and centred 0.52 m along its
measured outward normal. The new pane/frame/ribbon backs are beyond 0.62 m;
the facing front is at 0.57 m. These are display-layer clearances, not surveyed
cladding thicknesses. They hide the inappropriate old relief locally while
preserving the complete earlier sources, geometry and irregular roof.

identifies the Pfefferberg complex at Christinenstraße 18–19 / Schönhauser
Allee 176. Its entry `09095422,T,014` lists the 1911 shop floor with terrace
structure, credited to Atelier Zimmerreimer. That official attribution is kept
distinct from the Commons category's Salvisberg association.
The [theatre's history](https://pfefferberg-theater.de/unser-haus/historie/)

[the current Schankhalle operator](https://schankhalle-pfefferberg.de/) supplies
present-day use. [visitBerlin](https://www.visitberlin.de/de/pfefferberg)
confirms the entrance steps and elevated beer garden. No commercial site plan
or unlicensed photograph is traced.

## Pfefferberg scope and remaining limitation

The additions distinguish low hall openings and their dark frames/pale peach
surrounds, pale restaurant cornices, and brick-coloured hostel piers. Small
upper wall fragments receive shallow lips, not invented full-height windows.
The terrace receives shallow pilaster/capital and cornice profiles on its
existing envelope. Unsurveyed bay spacing, colours and member sections remain
procedural display estimates.

**This is not a complete reconstruction of Pfefferberg's terrain or open
terrace.** Its earlier outer model flattens building grounds to `y=3`, and the
terrace parent remains a coarse closed envelope. The neighbouring Weinberg
terrain correction does not cover this site. The source evidence retains the
mapped 13-step entry (`184595081`), two 12-step flights (`184595078`,
`184595085`), the last step/passage (`672103208`) and beer garden
(`80578266`). They are not rendered as floating flights or an unsupported new
raised plate. Opening the colonnade and making the approach genuinely
traversable would require a separate coordinated terrain, envelope and
collision correction. This release adds neither a false dark sky opening nor
a claim that this unresolved limitation is fixed.

## Runtime budgets and verification

Each site has a separate cullable bound. Compact buffers allocate only the final
count; every material has the existing day/night metadata and every transform
is frozen. No live building-residency, transfer, decoded-packet or CPU/GPU
ceiling changes. The runtime source JSON is 18,164 bytes; the complete bounded
source evidence is 518,311 bytes and is not loaded by the viewer.

`tests/test_suhrkamp_pfefferberg_v189.py` checks all eight identities, finite
bounds, source hashes, actual wall containment, the exact retained Suhrkamp
part/point inventory and evidence-only stair treatment. The Bun test constructs
and disposes both variants, validates the exact counts and buffer limits,
checks native matrices are axis-aligned, no UV/texture allocation, the site
bounds and full static drawn detail. A focused overlap regression checks all
ten Suhrkamp facing normals, wall-relative height/width and the positive
clearance of every new detail in front of its facing. Focused results:
**4 Python + 3 Bun tests
passed**. The root integration performs the complete release build and browser
before/after review.

Reproduce the runtime from committed evidence:

```bash
uv run python scripts/build_suhrkamp_pfefferberg_v189.py
uv run pytest tests/test_suhrkamp_pfefferberg_v189.py -q
cd src/app
bun test tests/suhrkamp-pfefferberg-v189.test.ts
```
