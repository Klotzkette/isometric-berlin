# Stadtbad and fire station on Oderberger Straße

Step 10 adds street-facing facade relief to the two complete retained
building owners. The Berlin monument register identifies the Stadtbad at
Oderberger Straße 57–59 and the fire station and remise at nos. 24–25.
The OSM building footprint of the latter is way `105780950`.
[Stadtbad heritage record](https://denkmaldatenbank.berlin.de/daobj.php?obj_dok_nr=09065159) ·
[Fire station heritage record](https://denkmaldatenbank.berlin.de/daobj.php?obj_dok_nr=09065136)

The Stadtbad gains a pale sandstone street face, rows of arched glazing,
mezzanine relief, pilasters and a recessed-looking central Renaissance portal.
The fire station gains warm brick, the brick corbel course, two distinct tiers
of sash windows and five red vehicle gates with toplights. The relief uses the
existing rendered walls. The building and roof shapes remain the original
LoD2 generalization. Opening and ornament dimensions are visual estimates.
The procedural artwork contains no source photograph pixels. Photo references
are credited in the existing viewer ledger.
