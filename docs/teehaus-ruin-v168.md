# English Garden Teehaus — documented post-fire ruin

Step 10, v1.0.68. The Teehaus is anchored to OSM way `25500391` and the
complete Berlin LoD2 parents `DEBE01YYK0002QHT`, `DEBE01YYK0003VSX` and
`DEBE01YYK0003VOb`: six parts and 90 original boundary polygons. The older
24 × 11 m presentation box was offset from the actual surveyed footprint.
Only that box and these six exact old prism/voxel owners are superseded.
Unrelated English Garden paths, vegetation, water and monuments remain.

The [borough's April 2026 announcement](https://www.berlin.de/ba-mitte/aktuelles/pressemitteilungen/2026/pressemitteilung.1660471.php)
confirms the September 2024 fire, largely collapsed roof, and planned clearing
of fire debris and unsafe gable sections. It does **not** establish the
completed condition after those works. The model therefore explicitly shows
the **documented December 2025 post-fire ruin**, not a claimed October 2026
survey or an invented rebuilding.

Per-file credits are appended in both attribution manifests. No photographic
pixels or textures ship in the viewer.

The visible ruin has low open side walls, standing terminal gables, a
continuous five-window row across the main gable, the small round opening,
asymmetric entrance, two chimneys with continuous lower masonry, sparse
charred members, a small surviving thatch fragment and a temporary site fence.
The main interior stays open to the sky. Window layout, surviving wall height,
damage, chimney and fence dimensions are **photo-informed display estimates**.
They are not newly measured architectural coordinates. In particular, the
fragment and fire-damaged front reconcile a source/photo conflict; the old
survey must not silently become a fictitious intact roof again.

`geo_data/regierungsviertel/teehaus-ruin-v168-evidence.json` keeps every original
polygon and one explicit disposition per polygon. The generator does not edit
the source archive, old correction records or general building catalogue.
The four drawn styles share the same geometry on touch and desktop. Minecraft
has an independent quarter-metre orthogonal surface skin, with lossless row
merging and no solid interior fill. Two drawn batches and one native batch
are constructed in separate cancellable stages. The original source solids
are replaced only by exact footprint and height matches.
