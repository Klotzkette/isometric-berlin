# Springer, taz and the southern Friedrichstraße/Kochstraße — v272

The former coarse Axel-Springer tower is replaced by the two complete measured
high parts of official parent `DEBE02YY4000042k` and all five parts of the silver
1994 wing `DEBE02YY400005Wf`. The unrelated hospital Springergebäude from v215
is untouched. The existing low Springer-Passage is also untouched. The original
full CityGML archives retain every unselected campus part.

The old taz house (`DEBE02YY40000CBT`, two parts) remains at Rudi-Dutschke-Straße 23.
The current taz house is **Friedrichstraße 21**, official `DEBE00YY29Q000SR`, four
parts, with its diamond metal screen and glazed facade. Peter Lenk's *Friede sei
mit Dir* stays on the former house's east wall, at OSM node 7599829392. A compact
independently authored relief interpretation replaces the old generic marker;
it is not a reproduction of the artwork or an assertion of surveyed figure details.

All seven old prism records are retained unchanged in the evidence and yield
only to the required replacement. The thirteen complete selected measured parts,
157 surfaces and 300 roof/wall triangles are reproduced. Native geometry uses
independent orthogonal surface cells, not filled interiors or rotated smooth meshes.
Matched walking roof profiles replace the old collisions. Existing source heights
are retained despite differing published nominal tower heights (66 m in the
heritage reference, 78 m in the owner reference). Those nominal figures are not
used to rescale the measured geometry.

199 ordinary retained buildings along the southern Friedrichstraße, Kochstraße
and Rudi-Dutschke-Straße receive road-facing shopfronts and window/sill details.
Existing road courses, delivered curbs, crossings and all source volumes remain.
Unknown individual shop identities, colors, metal/window subdivisions and fine
artwork proportions are display estimates, not a street-by-street photographic
survey. No source photograph, video or raster texture is bundled.
