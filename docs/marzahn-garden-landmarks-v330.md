# Gärten der Welt landmarks (V330)

The source file `geo_data/regierungsviertel/marzahn-garden-landmarks-v330.json` records the park boundary, complete selected OpenStreetMap records, the reception hall's four Berlin LoD2 parts, mapped building and water owners, and small manual traces from official orthophotos. The traces use the same local metre frame as the packet compiler, with X east from EPSG:25833 easting 389500 and Z south from northing 5820000. Crop origins, resolution and digests are recorded under `sourceAssets`. No reference image is distributed with the viewer. [Park operator's plan](https://www.gaertenderwelt.de/besuch-planen/parkplan/) · [Berlin's 2026 TrueDOP service](https://gdi.berlin.de/services/wms/truedop_2026) · [Official Berlin orthophoto information](https://www.berlin.de/sen/stadt/stadtdaten/geoinformation/landesvermessung/geotopographie-atkis/dop-digitale-orthophotos/)

- **Oriental-Islamic Garden.** The replacement uses the four measured base footprints of the reception hall, where a lower corridor stays open beneath the copper roof and glazed dome. The site model includes the pink enclosure, covered column arcades, two longitudinal channels, two narrower cross channels, four planted beds, the octagonal fountain pavilion and an open eastern gate. The operator documents the wall and hall scale. The pavilion section, ornament and roof profiles are approximations registered to the orthophoto and the operator's exterior and courtyard photos. The LoD2 reception owner is `DEBE10YYS00005jf`. The distinct western service owner `DEBE10YYS0000Vlj` is retained. [Park operator](https://www.gaertenderwelt.de/welt-entdecken/themengaerten/orientalisch-islamischer-garten/) · [Operator's dimensions](https://www.gaertenderwelt.de/fileadmin/gaertenderwelt/PDFs_Veranstaltungen/gaertenderwelt_rallye_schulgruppen_macht-euch-schlau.pdf)
- **Japanese Garden.** The main gate and small viewing hut use footprints traced from TrueDOP. The Chaya (Nyo Sui Tei) is an open pavilion on its mapped footprint. The dry stone cascade and raked gravel sit beside the stepping stones. A separate pond and stream represent the actual wet part of the garden. The published route and aerial view establish the placement of these features. Rock and roof profiles, gravel raking, and minor widths are modelled approximations. [Operator's Japanese Garden route](https://www.gaertenderwelt.de/welt-entdecken/themengaerten/japanischer-garten/rundweg/)
- **Korean Garden.** The Kye Zeong pavilion, three covered gateways and pond follow mapped outlines. The four courts, their stone and tile walls, rock edges and entrance figures follow the registered site reference and operator imagery. Profiles and the height of the roof, walls and figures are approximate. [Park operator](https://www.gaertenderwelt.de/welt-entdecken/themengaerten/koreanischer-garten/)
- **Hedge maze and ground labyrinth.** The 2 m yew walls occupy the complement of the nine mapped path ways and centre paving within the hedge-maze outline. The path width is estimated from the aerial view. Both entrances are kept open, and the raised viewing platform is placed at its mapped location. Its steps are visual geometry. The adjacent circular stone labyrinth uses a public-domain historical Chartres pattern, registered to the actual paving footprint and entrance axis. Individual paving joints are approximate. [Park operator](https://www.gaertenderwelt.de/welt-entdecken/themengaerten/irrgarten-und-labyrinth/) · [Landscape architect Gruppe F](https://www.baunetz-architekten.de/gruppe-f/4731603/projekt/4732641) · [Chartres vector credited to Sebastián Asegurado after Nordisk familjebok](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Labyrinth_1_(from_Nordisk_familjebok).svg)

`scripts/marzahn_garden_landmarks_v330.py` returns triangle geometry and finite walking obstacles for the park packet builder. Drawn paving shares the DGM mesh planes. Native geometry is sampled independently into cardinal faces and uses the corresponding stepped terrain heights. Water overlays are modelled surfaces with mapped exclusions for walking. All shared terrain, packet clipping and publication are handled by the park pipeline.

Berlin's TrueDOP and LoD2 sources use [Datenlizenz Deutschland Zero 2.0](https://www.govdata.de/dl-de/zero-2-0). OpenStreetMap data are © OpenStreetMap contributors under [ODbL 1.0](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). The Chartres vector is public domain.
