# Berlin architectural and public-access refinements

Ordinary residential façades use quieter families of rendered plaster, masonry
and concrete tones. The 7,173 existing source-identified parts and the 1,969
additional Drawn parts are guard-matched by ID and retained source geometry.
This is an architectural colour estimate. Existing mapped materials and
individually authored façades take precedence. City West's Allianz tower gains
the stone bands across the fourteen storeys described by the [Landesdenkmalamt](https://denkmaldatenbank.berlin.de/daobj.php?obj_dok_nr=09096212).
The older vaulted glass, tracks, roof geometry and pedestrian owners remain.

The northwest deck of Elsenbrücke follows its mapped footprint and currently
open three-lane carriageway. Both existing temporary bridge assets are retained.
Oberbaumbrücke gains the two mapped brick tower drums and pointed roofs; small
ornament is estimated. Deck approaches follow the existing ground datum and
carry navigation based on the rendered curbs and rails. The prior packet
complements can be restored exactly. [Berlin bridge project](https://www.berlin.de/sen/uvk/mobilitaet-und-verkehr/infrastruktur/brueckenbau/elsenbruecke/)
· [Oberbaumbrücke heritage record](https://denkmaldatenbank.berlin.de/daobj.php?obj_dok_nr=09095168)
· [OpenStreetMap attribution](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)

The sports compiler matches 213 mapped large football pitches, tennis courts
and tracks with positively tagged materials to existing flat ground. It clips
every surface to the ground polygons of its existing tile and excludes existing
roads, buildings, bridges and water. Close-fitting rectangular fields receive
paint based on common court proportions. Unmapped pitch markings and surfaces
remain uncertain, and horse-racing tracks without a supported mapped surface
are outside this selection. Native uses cardinal cells. The 234 changed files
retain all previous meshes and navigation.

The specific work at [Oderberger Straße](oderberger-facades.md), [Potsdamer
Platz](potsdamer-south-access.md), [Bahnhof Zoo](zoo-u9-public-connection.md),
[historical sites](prisons-memorials-v209.md) and [Jungfernheide](jungfernheide-climbing.md)
has separate source and model notes. At Hauptbahnhof, the six existing panoramic
shafts now identify the track pairs served above and below, based on the
[S-Bahn station equipment directory](https://sbahn.berlin/fahren/bahnhofsuebersicht/hauptbahnhof/).

At launch the site starts the scene index and one main map payload while the
renderer loads. The normal readers take over those responses and retain their
validation and retries. Packet URLs are restricted to the same directory as
their manifest. Both graphic families and all six modes are included.
